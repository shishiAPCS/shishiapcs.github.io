--- layout: post title: "新托福口语renewable energy 打开思路" date: 2026-09-17 categories: [ESL, TOEFL, Speaking] ---

这组题目都围绕 renewable energy，但四道题真正考的并不是同一种思路。 有的题需要表达观点，有的题需要讲经历，有的题需要观察现实，还有的题要求你比较利弊。 如果所有题都用同一种“观点 + 原因”的方式回答，答案很容易变得重复、空泛。

这一组题最值得记住的一句话：
Simple + clear + developed is usually better than complex + inaccurate.
简单、清楚、展开充分，通常比复杂但错误很多更好。

Question 1：先看三个答案，哪个更好？

Thanks for your participation. I'd like to discuss your views on renewable energy. To start, how important is the issue of renewable energy, such as wind and solar power, to you personally? Why would you say you feel that way?

这是一道典型的 Opinion Question。你需要先明确回答自己的看法，再解释原因，最好再加一个具体例子。

Version 1

I think it's very important to develop renewable energy, like wind power and solar power, especially in rural areas, where other forms of power are not easy to access. For example, in some areas in Southeast Asia, people live far away from cities, so they use solar power to generate electricity because there are no power plants near where they live.

So it's very important to develop these kinds of energy.

Version 2

I think renewable energy is essential in the foreseeable future, whether it's wind power, solar power, or any other source of clean energy. Because as we burn more coal and use more fossil fuels, they're going to be depleted in the end.

So we have to find alternatives. And in some places, people don't have coal to burn to generate electricity, nor do they have any, like, dams to use to generate electricity. Wind power and solar power would be a great choice.

Version 3

Well, I think renewable energy is essential, especially in the future, when there's not enough fossil fuel for us to use. Whether it's wind power or solar power, renewable energy makes it easy for people to have access to electricity.

Think about remote areas where people have no hydropower or coal to burn. Wind power and solar power would be very ideal ways for them to power up their entire house or apartment.

三个版本怎么比较？

版本 优点 主要问题
Version 1 逻辑最稳定；有具体场景；例子和观点直接相关 词汇和句型比较简单
Version 2 词汇更高级；涉及 fossil fuels、alternatives 等概念 想表达的内容太多，语言控制变弱；复杂度上升后更容易出错
Version 3 比较像真实聊天；“Think about...” 有口语感 部分表达不够自然，句子控制不如 Version 1 稳定
如果只看逐字稿：
Version 1 > Version 3 > Version 2

为什么 Version 1 最好？不是因为它用了更高级的词，而是因为它完成了一件更重要的事： 选了一个很具体的角度，然后把这个角度讲清楚。

Opinion → Reason → Example

Version 1 的思路其实非常简单：

renewable energy is important → remote areas need electricity → some places are far from power plants → solar power can help

整个回答一直沿着同一条线向前发展，没有突然切换话题。

常见误区：
很多同学会觉得“高级词越多，分数越高”。于是开始加入 fossil fuels、depletion、hydropower、infrastructure 等很多概念。 问题是：如果语言控制跟不上，答案反而会变得更难理解。

不要为了“显得高级”而同时讲太多东西。

第一题最适合的思维模型

OPINION → REASON → EXAMPLE

看到这种题，先问自己三个问题：

  1. What do I think? 我的观点是什么？
  2. Why? 最主要的一个原因是什么？
  3. Can I give one concrete example? 有没有一个具体例子？

Question 2：不要议论，讲一件具体发生过的事

Now, describe a time when you or someone you know tried to reduce energy use. What actions were taken and what made it easy or difficult?

这题和第一题已经完全不同了。 它不是让你继续解释“为什么节约能源很重要”，而是让你讲一个 experience。

Situation → Action → Difficulty → Result

可以把题目拆成四个小问题：

  1. When / Where? 什么时候？在哪里？
  2. What did you do? 具体采取了什么行动？
  3. What made it easy or difficult? 什么让它变得容易或困难？
  4. What happened in the end? 最后结果怎么样？

最容易找到的生活场景

场景 可以做什么 Easy / Difficult
夏天少开空调 set the AC to 26°C / use a fan 天气太热，很难坚持
离开房间关灯 turn off the lights 动作很简单，但容易忘
电脑、游戏机不用待机 turn them off completely 简单，但有时候觉得麻烦
短距离不开车 walk / bike / take public transport 天气好的时候容易，下雨时困难

怎么把思路“问出来”？

如果你一时想不到完整答案，不要逼自己马上组织一段话。先连续回答几个小问题：

What's the biggest thing that uses electricity in your home?
→ The air conditioner.

Have you ever tried to use it less?
→ Yes.

What did you do?
→ We set it to 26 degrees.

Was it easy?
→ Not really.

Why not?
→ Because it was extremely hot.

到这里，一个答案的骨架已经出来了。

这题最容易犯的错误：
看到 reduce energy use，就重新开始说： “Saving energy is very important because...”

这会把第二题说回第一题。
第二题的关键词是：describe a time。

Question 3：观察现实，再解释影响

Great. In your daily life, have you noticed any renewable energy initiatives in your community? If yes, what have you seen and how do they impact your area? If not, what's an initiative that might be beneficial for your community?

这题不是让你讲经历，而是让你说： 我在现实生活中看到了什么，以及它有什么作用。

而且这道题有两个分支。

如果你见过

WHAT → WHERE → IMPACT

比如你见过：

  • solar streetlights
  • solar panels on rooftops
  • solar-powered signs or cameras
  • wind turbines
  • solar water heaters

这时不要只停在：

There are some solar panels near my home.

因为题目后面还有更重要的一部分：

How do they impact your area?

可以从这些影响入手：

  • save electricity
  • lower energy costs
  • reduce pollution
  • provide power in remote areas
  • make the community greener

如果你没见过

IDEA → PLACE → BENEFIT

这时不需要设计什么复杂政策。选一个简单而具体的方案就够了：

install solar panels on school buildings

然后继续问：

  • Where should they be installed?
  • Why there?
  • Who would benefit?

例如：

on the roof of our school
→ it gets a lot of sunlight
→ the school can generate some of its own electricity
→ it may reduce electricity costs

这题的关键：
“看到什么”只是前半部分。
真正要继续展开的是：So what? What difference does it make?

Question 4：不是列优缺点，而是做比较

Thought-provoking. Many believe transitioning to renewable energy is crucial for environmental sustainability, while others worry about the costs involved. Do you think the benefits of renewable energy outweigh the drawbacks? Why or why not?

这题和第一题一样，都需要表达观点。 但它多了一层：你必须处理一个 trade-off，也就是“好处和坏处之间的权衡”。

Position → Benefit → Drawback → Why the benefit matters more

先不要列很多点

最简单的做法是只抓住：

  • 一个主要 benefit
  • 一个主要 drawback
  • 最后解释为什么其中一边更重要
Benefit Drawback 比较方式
less pollution expensive to build long-term environmental benefit
reduce fossil fuel use high initial cost fossil fuels are limited
lower energy costs later installation is expensive pay more first, save later
cleaner air solar and wind are not always stable different energy sources can work together

最容易展开的一组

high initial cost vs. long-term benefit

这个思路很自然：

Yes, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
→ The main benefit is less pollution.
→ Of course, renewable energy can be expensive to build at first.
→ But the cost is mostly short-term, while the environmental benefits can last for many years.

两个常见问题：

1. 只讲 benefits，不讲 drawbacks。
这样没有真正回应 outweigh。

2. 只是列清单。
“It is clean. It is renewable. It is expensive. It needs space.”
这只是 pros and cons，不是比较。

最后：四道题，其实是四种不同的思维模型

题目 题型 最适合的思路
Q1 Opinion Opinion → Reason → Example
Q2 Experience Situation → Action → Difficulty → Result
Q3 Observation / Solution What → Where → Impact
或 Idea → Place → Benefit
Q4 Evaluation Position → Benefit → Drawback → Why one matters more
真正要练的不是“背四个答案”，而是看到问题后知道自己应该用哪一种思路。

如果你能先判断题型，再用合适的结构打开思路，回答通常会更快、更清楚，也更容易继续展开。

注：上面的比较主要基于逐字稿本身。真实口语表现还会受到语速、停顿、发音、节奏和现场表达流畅度的影响。
