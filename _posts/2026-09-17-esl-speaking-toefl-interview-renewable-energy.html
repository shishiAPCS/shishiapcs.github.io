这组题都围绕 renewable energy，但四道题要求你做的事情并不一样： 有的题要表达观点，有的题要讲经历，有的题要观察现实，还有的题要比较两边。 先识别题型，再选择思路。

这一组题的核心原则：
Simple + clear + developed.
先把一个清楚的想法讲完整，再增加语言复杂度。
Q1 · OPINION

Question 1：先看三个答案，你会选哪一个？

Thanks for your participation. I'd like to discuss your views on renewable energy. To start, how important is the issue of renewable energy, such as wind and solar power, to you personally? Why would you say you feel that way?

先读三个版本。点击 Version 切换答案。读完以后，先做一次自己的判断，再看分析。

I think it's very important to develop renewable energy, like wind power and solar power, especially in rural areas, where other forms of power are not easy to access. For example, in some areas in Southeast Asia, people live far away from cities, so they use solar power to generate electricity because there are no power plants near where they live.

So it's very important to develop these kinds of energy.
I think renewable energy is essential in the foreseeable future, whether it's wind power, solar power, or any other source of clean energy. Because as we burn more coal and use more fossil fuels, they're going to be depleted in the end.

So we have to find alternatives. And in some places, people don't have coal to burn to generate electricity, nor do they have any, like, dams to use to generate electricity. Wind power and solar power would be a great choice.
Well, I think renewable energy is essential, especially in the future, when there's not enough fossil fuel for us to use. Whether it's wind power or solar power, renewable energy makes it easy for people to have access to electricity.

Think about remote areas where people have no hydropower or coal to burn. Wind power and solar power would be very ideal ways for them to power up their entire house or apartment.

你的选择：哪个版本最强？

如果只看逐字稿：Version 1 ＞ Version 3 ＞ Version 2

Version 1 最强的地方，是它始终沿着一条清楚的逻辑线展开： renewable energy 很重要 → 偏远地区需要电力 → 当地离传统电厂很远 → solar power 可以解决这个问题。

一个中心 整段只发展一个主要理由，所以容易跟上。
一个具体场景 Southeast Asia、remote areas、far from cities，让理由变得具体。
语言控制稳定 句型没有刻意变复杂，因此更容易保持准确、清楚和流畅。

把这道题压缩成三个问题

Opinion → Reason → Example
  1. What do I think? — 我对这个话题的态度是什么？
  2. Why? — 哪一个理由最容易解释？
  3. What's one concrete example? — 什么现实场景可以把这个理由讲清楚？
Version 1 的思路：
renewable energy is important → remote areas need electricity → power plants may be far away → solar power can help
Q2 · EXPERIENCE

Question 2：先找一个生活场景，再讲成一个小故事

Now, describe a time when you or someone you know tried to reduce energy use. What actions were taken and what made it easy or difficult?

这题需要一段具体经历。先找到一个熟悉的小场景，再按四步把故事搭起来。

1. Situation 什么时候？在哪里？
2. Action 具体做了什么？
3. Difficulty 什么让它容易或困难？
4. Result 最后怎么样？

选一个你最熟悉的场景

Air conditioner
Situation: It was a very hot summer.
Action: We set the AC to 26°C and used a fan more often.
Difficulty: It became difficult on extremely hot days.
Result: We still managed to use the AC less most of the time.

先把四格想清楚，再把四格连成一段自然口语。你不需要一开始就“想出完整答案”。

Q3 · OBSERVATION / SOLUTION

Question 3：先选择你的路线

Great. In your daily life, have you noticed any renewable energy initiatives in your community? If yes, what have you seen and how do they impact your area? If not, what's an initiative that might be beneficial for your community?

题目已经给了两条路线。选择更符合你情况的一条，然后继续展开。

路线 A：WHAT → WHERE → IMPACT

What? Solar streetlights / rooftop solar panels / solar-powered signs

Where? Near my neighborhood / on school buildings / along some roads

Impact? 选择一个最容易解释的实际影响：

save electricity lower energy costs reduce pollution provide local power

把描述继续推进一步

说完 “There are some solar panels on buildings near my home.” 后， 继续问自己： What difference do they make?

这个问题会把答案自然带到题目的核心：impact。

Q4 · EVALUATION

Question 4：选一边，再做一次真正的权衡

Thought-provoking. Many believe transitioning to renewable energy is crucial for environmental sustainability, while others worry about the costs involved. Do you think the benefits of renewable energy outweigh the drawbacks? Why or why not?
Position → Benefit → Drawback → Why one matters more

选择一个主要 benefit，再选择一个主要 drawback。页面会把它们连成一条完整的比较逻辑。

Choose one main benefit

Choose one main drawback

Your logic line

I think the benefits outweigh the drawbacks. Renewable energy can reduce pollution. Of course, it can be expensive to build at first, but the cost is mainly short-term while the environmental benefits can last for many years.

这里真正要完成的是 compare： 先承认另一边的代价，再解释为什么你的主要理由更重要。

最后：四道题，四张思维卡

1 Opinion
Opinion → Reason → Example
2 Experience
Situation → Action → Difficulty → Result
3 Observation / Solution
What → Where → Impact
Idea → Place → Benefit
4 Evaluation
Position → Benefit → Drawback → Why one matters more

表达观点？讲经历？描述观察？还是比较两边？
题型判断清楚以后，再用对应的结构打开思路。
