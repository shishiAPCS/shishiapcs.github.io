      <section class="ielts-section" id="bilingual-sample">
        <h2 class="ielts-section-title">3. 双语范文 <small>BILINGUAL SAMPLE</small></h2>

        <div class="ielts-lang-toolbar" role="group" aria-label="Language display">
          <button class="ielts-lang-btn" type="button" data-lang="both" aria-pressed="true">EN + 中文</button>
          <button class="ielts-lang-btn" type="button" data-lang="en" aria-pressed="false">English only</button>

        <div class="ielts-bilingual-block">
          <p class="lang-en">One of the most frustrating technological problems I've ever faced happened a few years ago when I was at home trying to snag tickets to a Taylor Swift concert. I was doing it by myself, but thousands of other fans were trying to get tickets at exactly the same time, which was probably why the system was under so much pressure.</p>
          <p class="lang-zh">我遇到过最让人抓狂的科技问题之一，是几年前我在家抢 Taylor Swift 演唱会门票的时候。当时我自己一个人在操作，但同时有成千上万的粉丝都在抢票，这大概就是系统压力那么大的原因。</p>
        </div>

        <div class="ielts-bilingual-block">
          <p class="lang-en">It was a huge deal for me because I'm a massive Taylor Swift fan. I was hoping to get a VIP package so I could sit relatively close to the stage and really experience the atmosphere up close.</p>
          <p class="lang-zh">这件事对我来说非常重要，因为我是 Taylor Swift 的超级粉丝。我希望能抢到 VIP 套票，这样就能坐得离舞台比较近，真正近距离感受现场气氛。</p>
        </div>

        <div class="ielts-bilingual-block">
          <p class="lang-en">The problem was that the moment ticket sales opened, the website basically collapsed under the traffic. The page kept freezing, sometimes it wouldn't load at all, and whenever I refreshed it, I either got an error message or ended up staring at the same blank screen. I couldn't even get into the booking system, let alone actually buy a ticket.</p>
          <p class="lang-zh">问题是，售票一开始，网站就因为访问量太大几乎崩溃了。页面不停卡住，有时候根本加载不出来；每次刷新，要么出现错误提示，要么还是同一个空白页面。我连购票系统都进不去，更别说真的买票了。</p>
        </div>

        <div class="ielts-bilingual-block">
          <p class="lang-en">At first, I just kept refreshing, but that clearly wasn't getting me anywhere. So I did some basic troubleshooting. I opened the site in another browser and switched from Wi-Fi to my phone's mobile hotspot, just in case my connection was part of the problem.</p>
          <p class="lang-zh">一开始我只是不停刷新，但很明显这样根本没有进展。所以我做了一些基本的故障排查：换了一个浏览器打开网站，还把 Wi-Fi 切换成手机热点，以防问题出在自己的网络连接上。</p>
        </div>

        <div class="ielts-bilingual-block">
          <p class="lang-en">After about five incredibly stressful minutes, the booking page finally loaded. I quickly chose the VIP package, entered my payment details, and paid by credit card. When the confirmation page appeared, I was absolutely thrilled and incredibly relieved.</p>
          <p class="lang-zh">大约过了极其煎熬的五分钟，购票页面终于加载出来了。我赶紧选择 VIP 套票，输入付款信息，并用信用卡完成支付。当确认页面出现时，我简直欣喜若狂，同时也如释重负。</p>
        </div>

        <div class="ielts-bilingual-block">
          <p class="lang-en">Looking back, five minutes doesn't sound very long, but when thousands of people are competing for a limited number of tickets, it feels like forever. After all that frustration, actually snagging the tickets felt incredibly satisfying, and luckily, I eventually got to see Taylor Swift live.</p>
          <p class="lang-zh">现在回头看，五分钟听起来并不长，但当成千上万的人都在抢数量有限的门票时，那感觉简直像等了一个世纪。经历了那么多挫折之后，真的抢到票特别有成就感；幸运的是，我最后也真的去现场看到了 Taylor Swift。</p>
        </div>
      </section>

      <section class="ielts-section" id="adapt-your-story">
        <h2 class="ielts-section-title">5. 一故事多用 <small>ADAPT YOUR STORY</small></h2>

        <div class="ielts-card ielts-adapt-intro">
          <strong>核心方法：</strong>不要背 12 个故事。保留同一个 Taylor Swift 世界，只改变故事的 <strong>起点、重点和结尾</strong>。
        </div>

        <div class="ielts-adapt-grid">
          <details class="ielts-adapt-card" open>
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-strong">强适配</span><strong>遇到的科技问题</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe a challenging technological problem you faced</span></summary>
            <div class="ielts-adapt-body">原故事直接使用：website crashes → troubleshooting → booking page loads → tickets secured。</div>
          </details>

          <details class="ielts-adapt-card">
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-adapt">可改编</span><strong>电子设备问题</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe a time when you had a problem with using an electronic device</span></summary>
            <div class="ielts-adapt-body">把问题从 website 改成 laptop / phone 在关键时刻 freeze；最后换设备完成购票。</div>
          </details>

          <details class="ielts-adapt-card" open>
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-strong">强适配</span><strong>为特别的东西存钱</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe something special you saved money to buy</span></summary>
            <div class="ielts-adapt-body">把重点提前：几个月一直为 VIP package 存钱；抢票故障只作为购买过程中的波折。</div>
          </details>

          <details class="ielts-adapt-card" open>
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-strong">强适配</span><strong>重要决定 + 好结果</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe an important decision that led to a positive result</span></summary>
            <div class="ielts-adapt-body">重点讲决定继续尝试 / 换网络，而不是放弃；结果是成功买票并看到演唱会。</div>
          </details>

          <details class="ielts-adapt-card" open>
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-strong">强适配</span><strong>改变计划</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe a time when you had to change your plan</span></summary>
            <div class="ielts-adapt-body">original plan：laptop + home Wi-Fi；出问题后改用 another browser / mobile hotspot / phone。</div>
          </details>

          <details class="ielts-adapt-card">
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-adapt">可改编</span><strong>好的购物 / 客服体验</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe a good shopping experience / good customer service</span></summary>
            <div class="ielts-adapt-body">在付款后加入 confirmation email 没收到 → 联系客服 → 对方快速核对订单并重新发送确认。</div>
          </details>

          <details class="ielts-adapt-card" open>
            <summary><span class="ielts-fit ielts-fit-strong">强适配</span><strong>和朋友度过的愉快夜晚</strong><span class="ielts-topic-en">Describe an enjoyable evening you spent with friends</span></summary>
