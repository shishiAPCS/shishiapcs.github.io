IELTS SPEAKING PART 2 · MASTER STORY · 2026 SEP–DEC

An Unexpected Discovery

新加坡的意外发现 · 滨海湾金沙 · 当童年记忆走进现实

Original Question · 原题

Describe a tall building you like or dislike.

You should say:

  • Where the place is
  • When and why you went there
  • What the place was like
  • And explain how you felt about the place

先讲好一次旅行中的意外发现：奇特建筑、动画回忆、临时改变计划、登顶看夜景与恐高的反差。最后用 serendipity（不期而遇的美好）与 nostalgia（怀旧之情）收束故事。

1核心故事
10关联话题（含拓展）
5核心句
25可复用表达

故事地图

先记住故事的发展顺序，点击任意步骤查看关键表达和可迁移话题。练习时只看关键词，不背完整句子。

故事主线
家庭旅行 → 偶遇建筑 → 想起动画 → 临时绕道 → 登顶赏夜景 → 恐高与拍照 → 怀旧与意外收获
双重情感主线：Serendipity 解释为什么这次经历令人惊喜；nostalgia 解释为什么它对你有特别的意义。

完整答案 · 中英对照

完整母素材（308 词）。保留最终确认的英文版本。正式考试请根据两分钟时限和题目重点灵活取舍。

01

Well, one building that immediately springs to mind is this rather unusual skyscraper complex in Singapore. It's one of the city's most iconic landmarks, although its name escapes me at the moment. I actually stumbled across it during a family trip last summer.

嗯，我脑海中立刻浮现的是新加坡一座相当独特的摩天大楼建筑群。它是当地最具标志性的地标之一，不过我一时想不起它的名字。去年夏天和家人旅行时，我其实是偶然发现它的。

02

One evening, we were making our way back to our hotel when this enormous structure caught my eye. It looks like a gigantic cruise ship perched on top of three skyscrapers.

一天晚上，我们正走回酒店，突然这座庞大的建筑吸引了我的注意。它看起来就像一艘巨型游轮架在三座摩天大楼的顶部。

03

It looked strangely familiar. I couldn't quite put my finger on it at first, but then it suddenly dawned on me that it had been featured in a Japanese animated movie. I've been a fan of that animated series since childhood, so you can imagine my excitement.

它看起来莫名熟悉。起初我说不上来为什么，但后来突然意识到，它曾出现在一部日本动画电影里。我从小就是那个动画系列的粉丝，所以你可以想象我当时有多兴奋。

04

On the spur of the moment, we decided to take a little detour and explore the building. We eventually made our way up to the rooftop observation deck, where we were treated to a breathtaking panoramic view of the city.

我们一时兴起，决定稍微绕个路去探索这座建筑。最后我们登上了屋顶观景台，有幸欣赏到令人惊叹的城市全景。

05

Just imagine — the entire skyline was illuminated, with countless lights stretching out as far as the eye could see. For a moment, we just stood there, soaking it all in.

想象一下——整个城市天际线都被灯光点亮，无数灯火一直延伸到视线的尽头。那一刻，我们就静静站在那里，尽情感受眼前的一切。

06

I'm actually quite uncomfortable with heights, so looking straight down made me feel slightly uneasy. It was a strange mixture of excitement and fear. But the spectacular scenery made it well worth the trip, and we ended up taking loads of pictures to capture the moment.

其实我不太适应高处，所以直直往下看让我有些不安。那是一种兴奋与恐惧交织的奇妙感觉。不过壮观的景色让这趟行程非常值得，最后我们拍了很多照片来记录这一刻。

07

Looking back, what made the experience particularly special was that none of it had been planned. Sometimes, the most rewarding experiences happen when you least expect them.

回想起来，这段经历之所以特别，是因为这一切都没有事先计划。有时候，最有收获的经历恰恰发生在你最意想不到的时候。

08

Seeing a little piece of my childhood come to life filled me with an overwhelming sense of nostalgia. It's a feeling that's genuinely hard to put into words.

看到童年记忆中的一小部分在现实中变得鲜活，我心中涌起强烈的怀旧之情。那种感觉真的难以用言语表达。

09

For me, it was one of those rare moments of serendipity that make traveling so worthwhile.

对我来说，那是一次难得的不期而遇的美好，也正是这样的时刻让旅行如此值得。

实战提示：原题优先交代建筑外观、登顶原因和感受。两分钟练习时可压缩动画背景及末尾感悟，但保留情绪反差。名称可直接说 Marina Bay Sands。

语言积累

先练五个核心句，再点击表达卡片查看中文释义和可迁移例句。

5 个核心句 · Anchor Sentences

01

Well, one building that immediately springs to mind is this rather unusual skyscraper complex in Singapore.

自然开场：迅速引出地点，也可替换 building 和地点。

02

I couldn't quite put my finger on it at first, but then it suddenly dawned on me that it had been featured in a Japanese animated movie.

先制造悬念，再交代恍然大悟的原因。

03

On the spur of the moment, we decided to take a little detour and explore the building.

引出临时决定和计划改变。

04

It was a strange mixture of excitement and fear. But the spectacular scenery made it well worth the trip.

用情绪反差让经历更具体。

05

Sometimes, the most rewarding experiences happen when you least expect them.

从个人经历上升到可迁移的人生感悟。

25 个可复用表达 · Reusable Chunks

spring to mind

浮现在脑海中；自然引出人、地、事或物

One experience that immediately springs to mind is my first school trip.

its name escapes me

一时想不起它的名字；escape 也可指某事未被记住

I remember the song, but its title escapes me at the moment.

stumble across something

偶然发现某物；并非刻意寻找

I stumbled across an old photo while cleaning my room.

catch someone's eye

吸引某人的注意

A colorful painting caught my eye.

be perched on top of

位于……顶端，仿佛架在上面

A tiny café was perched on top of a hill.

look strangely familiar

看起来莫名熟悉

The street looked strangely familiar.

put my finger on it

准确说出原因；常用于否定句

Something felt different, but I couldn’t quite put my finger on it.

it suddenly dawned on me that…

突然意识到……；后接从句

It suddenly dawned on me that I had left my keys at home.

be featured in

出现在节目、电影或报道中

The restaurant was featured in a travel documentary.

on the spur of the moment

一时兴起；没有预先计划

We booked the tickets on the spur of the moment.

take a little detour

稍微绕道；偏离原定路线

We took a little detour to visit a local market.

make our way up to

一路向上前往某处

We made our way up to the hilltop.

be treated to a breathtaking view

有幸欣赏到令人惊叹的景色

At sunrise, we were treated to a breathtaking view of the mountains.

as far as the eye could see

目之所及；放眼望去

There were fields of flowers as far as the eye could see.

soak it all in

充分感受景色、氛围与情绪

I sat by the sea for a while, soaking it all in.

feel slightly uneasy

感到有些不安

I felt slightly uneasy before the interview.

a mixture of excitement and fear

兴奋与恐惧交织

Before my first performance, I felt a mixture of excitement and fear.

be well worth the trip

非常值得专程前往；worth 后接名词或动名词

The exhibition was well worth the trip.

end up doing something

最终做了某事；注意接 -ing

We ended up spending the entire afternoon there.

capture the moment

记录下这一刻

I took a quick photo to capture the moment.

when you least expect it

在最意想不到的时候；注意单复数代词

Good opportunities sometimes come when you least expect them.

come to life

变得鲜活；仿佛走进现实

The illustrations made the story come to life.

an overwhelming sense of nostalgia

强烈的怀旧之情

Hearing that old song filled me with an overwhelming sense of nostalgia.

hard to put into words

难以用言语表达

My excitement was hard to put into words.

a moment of serendipity

一次意外的美好发现；不是普通的惊讶或倒霉巧合

Finding my favorite childhood book in a secondhand shop was a moment of serendipity.

发音重点

选择美式或英式英语，点击单词听读。音频使用浏览器系统语音；音标为常见美式参考，实际语音因设备而异。

区别：serendipity = 意外发现美好的事物；nostalgia = 怀念过去的情感。两个词不要混用。

话题迁移 · Adapt Your Story

以下方向根据本站 2026 年 9–12 月题库主题整理。原题可以直接使用，其他题目需改变重心或补充真实细节。英文标题为课堂练习措辞，不代表官方原题。

★ 直接适用

喜欢或不喜欢的高建筑

Describe a tall building you like or dislike

原题；地点、时间、原因、外观与感受都已覆盖。

答题路径：新加坡旅行 → 船形屋顶 → 动画回忆 → 登顶 → 复杂感受

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

One building that immediately springs to mind is this rather unusual skyscraper complex in Singapore.

示例结尾

For me, it was one of those rare moments of serendipity that make traveling so worthwhile.

↗ 换重心

喜欢去的建筑

Describe a building you enjoy visiting

以建筑本身和参观体验为重点。若题目要求经常去，需补真实的重复到访经历，不要把一次参观说成多次。

答题路径：建筑外形 → 为什么进去 → 观景台 → 夜景和感受

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

One building I really enjoyed visiting was an unusual skyscraper complex in Singapore.

示例结尾

What I enjoyed most was the combination of stunning architecture and a view I’ll never forget.

↗ 换重心

推荐旅行过的地方

Describe a place you have travelled to that you would like to recommend to others

已有旅行经历；补充适合什么游客、什么时候去、实际建议。

答题路径：新加坡 → 偶遇地标 → 夜景 → 推荐给谁 → 出行建议

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

If someone were visiting Singapore, I’d definitely recommend checking out Marina Bay Sands.

示例结尾

If you enjoy architecture and city views, it’s well worth a visit.

＋ 需拓展

近期改变的计划

Describe a plan that you had to change recently

原稿是自愿临时改计划，并非不得不改；也不一定满足 recently。只有真实经历中有近期发生的必要变动，才适合此题。

答题路径：原计划（回酒店）→ 必须改动的原因与时间（补）→ 新安排 → 结果

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

We had originally planned to head straight back to our hotel, but our plans changed.

示例结尾

In the end, the change of plans turned out to be a pleasant surprise.

＋ 需拓展

对结果开心的重要决定

Describe an important decision you made that you were happy with

临时观景不必然是重要决定。补真实的取舍、影响或意义，不要硬拔高。

答题路径：原计划 → 真正重要的选择及理由（补）→ 行动 → 结果

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

One decision I’m glad I made was to change our itinerary during a family trip.

示例结尾

Looking back, I’m glad I made that choice.

＋ 需拓展

改变重要想法

Describe a time when you changed an important opinion

原稿只反思旅行，未明确观点改变。若确实曾坚持严格行程、后来改变，补前后观点和意义。

答题路径：原来的观点（补）→ 偶遇建筑 → 感受 → 新观点

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

I used to think that a good trip had to be planned down to the last detail.

示例结尾

Now I’m more willing to leave room for unexpected discoveries.

＋ 需拓展

喜欢拍照的人

Describe a person you know who really likes taking photos

原稿只说全家拍照，未介绍摄影爱好者。需补真实人物、关系、拍摄习惯及一次具体表现。

答题路径：人物 → 摄影习惯（补）→ 新加坡夜景 → 具体拍摄 → 欣赏原因

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

Someone in my family really enjoys taking photos whenever we travel.

示例结尾

I admire the way this person captures moments we might otherwise forget.

＋ 需拓展

拥挤的地方

Describe a crowded place that you have visited

原稿没提拥挤；若真实有排队、人群等，补具体情景及影响。

答题路径：位置 → 到访 → 人群与排队（补）→ 对体验的影响 → 感受

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

One crowded place I remember visiting was a famous landmark in Singapore.

示例结尾

Despite the crowds, the view made the visit worthwhile.

＋ 需拓展

嘈杂的地方

Describe a noisy place you have been to

原稿没提噪音。只有实际遇到人声、音乐等噪音才使用。

答题路径：位置 → 到访 → 噪音来源（补）→ 感受 → 与夜景对比

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

A place that was particularly noisy during my trip was a busy tourist attraction in Singapore.

示例结尾

The view was beautiful, but the noise made it difficult to relax.

＋ 需拓展

有趣视频

Describe an interesting video you watched

动画电影不必然等于短视频；若确实看过相关有趣视频，补视频内容、观看经过和趣味。

答题路径：视频内容（补）→ 建筑首次印象 → 现实偶遇 → 为什么有趣

示例开头（以真实经历为准）

I once watched a video that showed an unusual building in Singapore.

示例结尾

It was especially interesting to see that place in real life later on.

母素材 · 不要机械套题
旅行 → 偶遇建筑 → 童年动画 → 临时决定 → 夜景 → 恐高与拍照 → 怀旧与意外之喜。没有发生的事不能从原文推断；人物题讲人物，事件题讲起因、经过、结果。
