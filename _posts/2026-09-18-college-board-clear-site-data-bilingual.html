AP / College Board · Troubleshooting

College Board 打开很慢，开无痕窗口反而很快？

先别急着换电脑，也不用把整个浏览器的数据全部清掉。 可以先只清理 College Board 自己保存的 Cookies 和网站数据， 让浏览器重新建立一次干净的登录会话。

典型信号：普通窗口慢 → 无痕窗口明显更快
先说明： 我们平时会简单地说“清一下 CB 缓存”，但这里更准确的说法是 清除 College Board 的 Cookies + Site Data（网站数据）。 这不保证网络本身会变快，但如果问题来自旧登录状态、过期会话或异常网站数据， 这样做经常可以解决登录慢、反复跳转或页面一直转圈的问题。

为什么无痕模式有时反而更快？

普通浏览器窗口

会继续使用之前留下的 Cookie、登录会话和网站本地数据。

无痕 / InPrivate / 私人浏览

相当于从一个比较“干净”的临时浏览环境开始，不会直接沿用普通窗口里的旧网站数据。

旧的 College Board 登录信息
↓
网站检查旧 Session / Cookie
↓
如果状态异常：可能重新验证、跳转、等待
↓
页面看起来就会特别慢

所以，如果你发现同一台电脑、同一个网络，无痕模式明显更快， 那么可以先尝试清理 College Board 的网站数据。

选择你的浏览器

点击下面的浏览器卡片，查看详细操作步骤。再次点击当前卡片可以收起。

Chrome 详细步骤

Chrome

打开“网站数据”页面

最直接的方法是在 Chrome 地址栏输入：

chrome://settings/content/all

如果这个地址在你的 Chrome 版本里跳转了，也可以从设置里进入：

Chrome 设置 → 隐私和安全 → 第三方 Cookie → 查看所有网站数据和权限

搜索 College Board

在网站数据页面搜索 collegeboard。

你可能会看到 collegeboard.org 或它的相关子域名。

删除 College Board 的网站数据

点击 College Board 条目旁边的删除 / 垃圾桶图标， 删除与 collegeboard.org 有关的网站数据。

注意：删除后，你会从 College Board 退出登录。这是正常现象。

关闭旧页面并重新登录

关闭所有 College Board、My AP、AP Classroom 标签页， 再重新打开 My AP 并登录。

Microsoft Edge 详细步骤

Edge

打开 Cookie 和网站数据设置

Edge 设置 → Cookie 和网站权限 → 管理和删除 Cookie 和站点数据 → 查看所有 Cookie 和网站数据

搜索 College Board

搜索 collegeboard， 找到 College Board 相关条目。

删除并重新登录

删除对应的 Cookie 和网站数据， 然后关闭所有 College Board / My AP / AP Classroom 标签页， 再重新打开并登录。

Safari（Mac）详细步骤

Safari

打开“管理网站数据”

Safari → 设置 → 隐私 → 管理网站数据

搜索并移除 College Board

搜索 collegeboard， 选中 College Board 相关网站数据后点击移除， 最后点击完成。

重新打开 College Board

关闭之前打开的 College Board 页面， 再用普通 Safari 窗口重新进入 My AP / AP Classroom 并登录。

这里指 Mac 版 Safari。 iPhone / iPad 的 Safari 设置路径不同。如果只是解决 College Board 的问题， 不要直接选择“移除所有网站数据”，否则会影响其他网站的登录状态。
三个浏览器最后都一样： 清完后关闭所有 College Board / My AP / AP Classroom 标签页， 再重新打开并登录。

这样做会删除什么？

  • College Board 的 Cookie
  • 之前保存的登录 / Session 信息
  • College Board 在浏览器本地保存的网站数据
  • 可能出现异常的旧网站状态

这样做不会影响什么？

  • 不会把其他网站一起退出登录
  • 不会删除整个浏览器的浏览历史
  • 不会删除书签
  • 不会删除浏览器密码管理器里保存的密码
  • 不会删除你的 College Board 账号、课程或 AP Classroom 作业
不要一上来就点“清除全部浏览数据”。 如果只是 College Board 有问题，优先只清 College Board。 这样改动最小，也不会把其他网站的登录状态一起清掉。

什么时候值得试？

✅ 值得先试

普通窗口很慢，但无痕窗口明显正常。

✅ 值得先试

登录后反复跳转、一直转圈，或者突然打不开 AP Classroom。

🤔 不一定有用

普通窗口和无痕窗口都一样慢。

🤔 不一定有用

全班所有设备同时变慢——这时更可能是网络或 College Board 服务本身的问题。

记住这个排查思路：
无痕快、普通慢 → 先怀疑浏览器里的旧网站数据。
先清 College Board 自己的数据，再重新登录，而不是先清整个浏览器。

参考：Google Chrome Help、Microsoft Support、Apple Support 关于按网站管理 Cookie / 网站数据的官方说明。
浏览器菜单名称可能会随版本更新略有变化，但原则不变：优先只删除 College Board 的网站数据，而不是清空整个浏览器。

AP / College Board · Troubleshooting

College Board slow in a regular window but faster in private browsing?

You probably do not need to switch computers or wipe all of your browser data. A good first step is to clear only College Board's cookies and site data so the browser can start a fresh login session.

A useful clue: regular window is slow → private window is noticeably faster
One quick clarification: people often call this “clearing the College Board cache,” but what we are really doing here is removing College Board cookies and site data. This will not make your internet connection itself faster. However, if the slowdown is caused by an expired login session, stale cookies, or problematic local site data, it can fix slow sign-ins, repeated redirects, or pages that keep spinning.

Why can private browsing feel faster?

Regular browser window

It keeps using cookies, login sessions, and local site data left over from earlier visits.

Incognito / InPrivate / Private Browsing

It starts with a much cleaner temporary browser state instead of reusing most of that existing site data.

Old College Board login data
↓
College Board checks an existing session or cookie
↓
If something is stale: extra checks, redirects, or delays may happen
↓
The page feels unusually slow

So if the same computer on the same network is much faster in private browsing, clearing College Board's site data is a sensible first troubleshooting step.

Choose your browser

Click a browser card to open the detailed instructions. Click the same card again to collapse them.

Chrome: detailed steps

Chrome

Open the site-data page

The quickest method is to enter this in the Chrome address bar:

chrome://settings/content/all

If Chrome redirects that address in your version, you can also go through Settings:

Chrome Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → See all site data and permissions

Search for College Board

Search for collegeboard on the site-data page.

You may see collegeboard.org along with related College Board subdomains.

Remove College Board site data

Use the delete / trash icon next to the College Board entries and remove the data associated with collegeboard.org.

Note: this will sign you out of College Board. That is expected.

Close the old tabs and sign in again

Close every College Board, My AP, and AP Classroom tab. Then reopen My AP and sign in again.

Microsoft Edge: detailed steps

Edge

Open cookies and site-data settings

Edge Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data → See all cookies and site data

Search for College Board

Search for collegeboard and find the College Board entries.

Delete the data and sign in again

Remove the College Board cookies and site data. Then close every College Board / My AP / AP Classroom tab, reopen the site, and sign in again.

Safari (Mac): detailed steps

Safari

Open Manage Website Data

Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Find and remove College Board

Search for collegeboard, select the College Board entries, click Remove, and then click Done.

Reopen College Board

Close the College Board pages you already had open. Then open My AP / AP Classroom again in a regular Safari window and sign in.

This section is for Safari on Mac. Safari on iPhone and iPad uses a different settings path. If College Board is the only site having trouble, avoid removing all website data, because that can sign you out of other websites too.
The final step is the same in all three browsers: after clearing the site data, close every College Board / My AP / AP Classroom tab, reopen the site, and sign in again.

What will this remove?

  • College Board cookies
  • Previously saved login and session information
  • College Board site data stored locally by the browser
  • Potentially stale or broken site state

What will this NOT affect?

  • It will not sign you out of unrelated websites
  • It will not erase your full browsing history
  • It will not delete your bookmarks
  • It will not delete passwords saved in your browser's password manager
  • It will not delete your College Board account, courses, or AP Classroom assignments
Do not start by clearing all browsing data. If College Board is the only site having trouble, remove College Board's data first. It is a much smaller change and avoids signing you out of unrelated sites.

When is this worth trying?

✅ Good time to try it

The regular window is slow, but private browsing works normally.

✅ Good time to try it

College Board keeps redirecting, spinning, or suddenly refuses to load AP Classroom.

🤔 It may not help

Both regular and private windows are equally slow.

🤔 It may not help

Everyone in the class becomes slow at the same time. That points more toward the network or the College Board service itself.

Remember the troubleshooting pattern:
Private browsing is fast, regular browsing is slow → suspect old browser site data first.
Clear College Board's own data and sign in again before wiping the whole browser.

References: official Google Chrome Help, Microsoft Support, and Apple Support documentation on managing cookies and website data by site.
Menu labels can change slightly between browser versions, but the principle stays the same: remove College Board's data first instead of clearing the entire browser.
