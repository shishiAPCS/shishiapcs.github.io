Tony Hawk: IELTS Speaking Part 2 Ingredient Sheet

Section 1: Basic Introduction

1. Basic Introduction

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
Tony Hawk is an American professional skateboarder, entrepreneur, and one of the most recognizable figures in skateboarding. Tony Hawk 是美国职业滑板运动员、企业家，也是滑板运动中最有辨识度的人物之一。 a legendary professional skateboarder 传奇职业滑板运动员
He was born in San Diego, California, in 1968 and got his first skateboard when he was nine. 他 1968 年出生于美国加州圣迭戈，9 岁时得到了人生第一块滑板。 get into something at a young age 很小的时候就开始接触某件事
He turned professional at 14 and became one of the strongest competitive skateboarders of his generation. 他 14 岁转为职业滑板手，并逐渐成为那个时代最顶尖的竞技滑板选手之一。 turn professional at a young age 很年轻时就转为职业选手
His career later expanded into business, video games, media, and charity. 后来，他的事业从滑板扩展到商业、电子游戏、媒体和公益领域。 more than just an athlete 不只是运动员
His story is useful for IELTS because it can be told as a sports story, a comeback story, a business story, or an inspiration story. 他的故事特别适合雅思 Part 2，因为既可以讲运动，也可以讲逆境翻身、商业成功或个人启发。 a story with many different angles 一个可以从很多角度讲的故事

2. Early Start: From Beginner to Professional

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
According to his official biography, his older brother gave him a used skateboard when he was nine. 根据他的官方传记，Tony Hawk 9 岁时哥哥送给他一块用过的滑板。 start with very little 从很普通的起点开始
He practiced at local skateparks and began winning amateur contests by the age of 12. 他经常在本地滑板公园练习，12 岁左右就开始赢得业余比赛。 practice consistently 持续稳定地练习
He turned professional at 14, which was unusually young. 他 14 岁就成为职业选手，这个年龄非常早。 show exceptional talent 展现出非凡天赋
By 16, he was already regarded as one of the best competitive skateboarders in the world. 到了 16 岁，他已经被视为世界上最顶尖的竞技滑板选手之一。 rise quickly through the ranks 迅速跻身顶尖行列
His early success came from both natural ability and thousands of hours of practice. 他的早期成功既来自天赋，也来自大量反复练习。 talent only takes you so far 只有天赋是不够的

3. Competitive Success: Records and Championships

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
By the time he was 25, his official biography says he had competed in 103 professional contests, winning 73 and finishing second in 19. 根据他的官方传记，到 25 岁时，他参加过 103 场职业比赛，其中 73 次夺冠、19 次获得第二名。 an extraordinary competition record 非常惊人的比赛战绩
He was a 12-time world champion in vertical skateboarding. 他曾连续多年统治 U 型池/垂直滑板项目，并成为 12 届世界冠军。 dominate a sport for years 多年统治一个运动项目
X Games lists him with 16 medals: 10 gold, three silver, and three bronze. X Games 的官方资料显示，他一共获得 16 枚 X Games 奖牌：10 金、3 银、3 铜。 a highly decorated athlete 获得大量荣誉的运动员
His success helped make vertical skateboarding more visible to mainstream audiences. 他的成功让更多主流观众开始认识和关注垂直滑板。 bring a niche sport into the mainstream 把小众运动带入主流视野
He became famous not just for winning, but for constantly trying difficult new tricks. 他出名不只是因为赢比赛，还因为不断挑战高难度的新动作。 keep pushing the limits 不断挑战极限

4. The 900: His Most Famous Moment

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
In 1999, at the X Games in San Francisco, Tony Hawk became the first skateboarder to land a 900. 1999 年，在旧金山 X Games 上，Tony Hawk 成为第一位成功完成 900 度旋转动作的滑板选手。 land a groundbreaking trick 完成一个突破性的高难度动作
A 900 means rotating two and a half full turns in the air before landing back on the ramp. 900 指的是在空中完成两圈半旋转，再重新落回坡面。 two and a half rotations in the air 在空中旋转两圈半
He had tried the trick for years and failed many times before finally landing it. 在成功之前，他为这个动作尝试了很多年，也失败过很多次。 fail repeatedly before succeeding 反复失败后最终成功
The moment became one of the most iconic events in X Games history. 这个瞬间后来成为 X Games 历史上最经典的时刻之一。 an iconic sporting moment 标志性的体育时刻
For IELTS, the 900 is a perfect mini-story about persistence, risk, pressure, and breakthrough. 对雅思来说，900 这个故事非常好用，因为它同时包含坚持、风险、压力和突破。 a breakthrough after years of practice 多年练习后的突破

5. Setback and Comeback: When Skateboarding Lost Popularity

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
In the early 1990s, skateboarding suddenly became much less popular, and Tony Hawk's income dropped dramatically. 20 世纪 90 年代初，滑板热度突然大幅下降，Tony Hawk 的收入也随之暴跌。 go through a difficult period 经历一段困难时期
His official biography says money became so tight that he lived on a very small daily food budget. 他的官方传记提到，当时经济状况非常紧张，甚至只能用很少的每日预算解决吃饭问题。 struggle financially 经济上陷入困境
Instead of leaving skateboarding, he believed the sport would recover and stayed committed to it. 但他没有离开滑板，而是相信这项运动会重新流行，并继续坚持。 stick with something through hard times 在困难时期仍坚持某件事
He refinanced his home to help launch Birdhouse, even though the company struggled in its early years. 为了创办 Birdhouse，他甚至重新贷款自己的房子，而公司最初几年也并不赚钱。 take a calculated risk 承担经过考虑的风险
When skateboarding became popular again, both his career and his business recovered strongly. 后来滑板重新流行时，他的职业生涯和商业事业也一起迎来了强劲反弹。 make a remarkable comeback 实现令人印象深刻的翻身

6. Business Success: Birdhouse and the Tony Hawk Brand

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
Tony Hawk co-founded Birdhouse in the early 1990s when the skateboard industry was struggling. Tony Hawk 在 20 世纪 90 年代初滑板行业低迷的时候共同创办了 Birdhouse。 start a business in a difficult market 在市场低迷时创业
Birdhouse survived the difficult early years and became a well-known skateboard company. Birdhouse 熬过了艰难的早期阶段，后来成为知名滑板公司。 build a brand from the ground up 从零开始打造品牌
He also developed businesses around clothing, sporting goods, media, licensing, and brand partnerships. 他还把事业扩展到服装、运动用品、媒体、授权和品牌合作等领域。 build multiple business ventures 发展多个商业项目
His name and reputation became valuable business assets, not just signs of sporting fame. 他的名字和声誉逐渐成为真正有价值的商业资产，而不只是体育名气。 turn reputation into commercial value 把声誉转化为商业价值
His business story shows how someone can turn a passion into a long-term career and brand. 他的商业故事说明，一个人可以把热爱慢慢发展成长期事业和个人品牌。 turn passion into a career 把热爱变成事业

7. Tony Hawk's Pro Skater: Video Games and Global Fame

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
In 1999, Tony Hawk teamed up with Activision to create the Tony Hawk's Pro Skater video game franchise. 1999 年，Tony Hawk 与 Activision 合作推出《Tony Hawk's Pro Skater》电子游戏系列。 team up with a major company 与大型公司合作
The games introduced skateboarding culture, tricks, music, and Tony Hawk himself to millions of players. 这些游戏把滑板文化、滑板动作、音乐以及 Tony Hawk 本人介绍给了数以百万计的玩家。 reach a much wider audience 触达更广泛的受众
Tony Hawk's official website says the game franchise has generated nearly two billion dollars in sales. Tony Hawk 官方网站称，这个游戏系列累计销售额接近 20 亿美元。 a hugely successful game franchise 非常成功的电子游戏系列
For many people, including the master story used on this website, the video games were their first introduction to skateboarding. 对很多人来说——包括本站已经使用的 master story——这些游戏就是他们第一次接触滑板文化的入口。 a gateway into a new hobby 进入一项新爱好的入口
This makes Tony Hawk unusually useful for IELTS topics about childhood memories, technology, games, hobbies, and personal influence. 这让 Tony Hawk 不仅能用于人物题，也能自然连接童年回忆、科技产品、电子游戏、兴趣和个人影响等话题。 connect childhood memories with adult interests 把童年记忆和成年后的兴趣连接起来

8. The Skatepark Project: Helping Young People

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
Tony Hawk founded the organization now called The Skatepark Project in 2002. Tony Hawk 在 2002 年创立了后来改名为 The Skatepark Project 的公益组织。 found a nonprofit organization 创办公益组织
Its mission is to help underserved communities create safe and inclusive public skateparks for young people. 这个组织的目标是帮助资源不足的社区，为年轻人建设安全、包容的公共滑板公园。 support underserved communities 帮助资源不足的社区
The organization says it has helped build close to 700 skateparks across all 50 U.S. states. 该组织目前表示，它已经帮助美国 50 个州建设了接近 700 个滑板公园。 create opportunities for young people 为年轻人创造机会
Hawk says he used prize money from a celebrity game show to help launch the nonprofit. Hawk 介绍说，他曾把参加名人电视答题节目获得的奖金用于帮助启动这个公益项目。 put money back into the community 把钱重新投入社区
This part of his story makes him a much stronger answer for 'a helpful person' than simply saying he inspires people. 这一部分让他回答“乐于助人的人”非常自然，因为这里有具体行动，而不只是抽象地说“他鼓励了别人”。 make a real difference in people's lives 真正改善别人的生活

9. Longevity, Persistence, and Lifelong Practice

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
Tony Hawk has remained involved in skateboarding for decades after turning professional as a teenager. Tony Hawk 十几岁成为职业选手后，几十年来一直没有真正离开滑板。 stay active for decades 活跃几十年
He has continued skating, appearing at events, and attempting difficult tricks long after his peak competition years. 即使巅峰竞技时期已经过去，他仍然继续滑板、参加活动并尝试高难度动作。 keep doing something for the love of it 因为真正热爱而一直坚持
This is why the Tony Hawk video story on this website focuses on lifelong practice rather than only medals. 所以本站的 Tony Hawk“有趣视频”故事重点不是奖牌，而是终身练习和不断进步。 keep learning and improving 持续学习和进步
His age makes the story more memorable because skateboarding is physically demanding and carries a real risk of injury. 这个故事的反差在于滑板对身体要求很高，而且确实有受伤风险，因此长期坚持更显得难得。 stay committed despite physical challenges 即使有身体挑战仍然坚持
For students, his story is a useful way to talk about discipline without sounding like a generic motivational speech. 对学生来说，用他的真实经历讲“自律和坚持”比空泛地说励志口号更自然。 lead by example 用行动做榜样

10. Influence, Media, and Public Image

Useful facts / 基本信息 IELTS-friendly phrases / 可借用英文表达 Chinese Meaning / 中文意思
Tony Hawk became famous far beyond skateboarding through television, films, books, games, and online media. Tony Hawk 的影响力早已超出滑板圈，还延伸到电视、电影、书籍、电子游戏和网络媒体。 cross over into popular culture 跨界进入大众文化
His autobiography became a New York Times bestseller, and he later created media projects through his production company, 900 Films. 他的自传曾登上《纽约时报》畅销书榜，后来他还通过自己的制作公司 900 Films 参与媒体项目。 tell his story through different media 通过不同媒体讲述自己的故事
He is often presented as approachable and humorous rather than as a distant celebrity. 他的公众形象通常比较亲切、幽默，不太像一个高高在上的名人。 come across as down-to-earth 给人脚踏实地、很亲切的感觉
His influence helped make skateboarding more visible and culturally accepted. 他的长期影响帮助滑板变得更大众，也让更多人把它看作一种真正的运动和文化。 change public perceptions 改变公众看法
That combination of sport, business, media, and social impact is why one Tony Hawk story can cover so many IELTS Part 2 questions. 正因为他的故事同时包含体育、商业、媒体和社会影响，一个 Tony Hawk 才能自然地覆盖很多 IELTS Part 2 题目。 a versatile master story 非常灵活的 master story

Section 2: IELTS Part 2 Topics

A. Strong Fit Topics

Chinese Topic Name English Topic Name How to Use / 如何使用
钦佩的运动员 Describe a successful sportsperson you admire Use Tony Hawk directly. Focus on his long competitive career, championships, the 900, and the fact that he kept skating for decades. 这个题可以直接用 Tony Hawk。重点讲他的长期职业生涯、冠军成绩、900 度动作，以及几十年来仍然坚持滑板。
遇到困难终成功的人 Describe a person who met difficulties but succeeded Use the early-1990s collapse in skateboarding: his income fell, he took a financial risk on Birdhouse, the company struggled, and he eventually made a major comeback. 可以讲 90 年代初滑板行业低迷：他的收入暴跌、冒险创办 Birdhouse、公司早期很困难，但后来随着滑板重新流行，他实现了强势翻身。
成功的生意人 Describe a person you know who has a successful business This is one of the strongest Tony Hawk topics. Explain Birdhouse, licensing, clothing, media, and especially how his name became a valuable personal brand. 这是 Tony Hawk 最适合的话题之一。可以讲 Birdhouse、品牌授权、服装、媒体，以及他如何把自己的名字经营成一个有价值的个人品牌。
想见的名人 Describe a famous person you would like to meet Use the existing website master story: you first knew his name through Tony Hawk's Pro Skater, later rediscovered him after getting into skateboarding, and would love to see him skate in person. 直接沿用本站已有 master story：小时候通过《Tony Hawk's Pro Skater》知道他的名字，后来自己开始玩滑板后重新认识他，希望有一天能亲眼看他滑板并和他聊几句。
有趣视频 Describe an interesting video Use a Tony Hawk skateboarding video. Focus on seeing him perform difficult tricks later in life and the lesson of continuing to practice and improve. 讲一个 Tony Hawk 的滑板视频。重点放在他年龄大了以后仍然做高难度动作，以及“只要还能做，就继续练习和进步”这个启发。
长久目标 Describe a long-term goal you would like to achieve Use the existing skateboarding goal story: become a much better skateboarder, inspired by Tony Hawk's games, videos, and long career. 直接使用本站“成为更好的滑板手”的故事：长期目标是提升滑板水平，灵感来自 Tony Hawk 的游戏、视频和长期坚持。
长远志向 Describe an ambition that you have had for a long time Use the childhood Tony Hawk game as the origin of your ambition to become a semi-professional skateboarder. Add effort, injury/setback, current reality, and why you still do not want to give it up. 把童年玩 Tony Hawk 游戏作为“想成为半职业滑板手”这个志向的起点，再讲练习、受伤、现实难度，以及为什么到现在仍不想放弃。
想看的体育赛事 Describe a sports event you would like to attend Talk about attending a major skateboarding competition or Tony Hawk's Vert Alert. Explain that you want to see elite tricks in person and experience the atmosphere with family or friends. 可以讲想去看大型滑板比赛，或者 Tony Hawk's Vert Alert。重点是亲眼看顶尖选手做动作、感受现场气氛，并和家人朋友一起体验。
乐于助人的人 Describe a person who often helps others Use The Skatepark Project. This gives you concrete evidence: Hawk founded a nonprofit that helps underserved communities build safe public skateparks for young people. 用 The Skatepark Project 来讲，这个角度非常扎实：Hawk 创办公益组织，帮助资源不足的社区为年轻人建设安全的公共滑板公园。
名人出演的广告 Describe an advertisement with a famous person in it Use a Tony Hawk sportswear, skateboard, game, or brand campaign. The key idea is credibility: the product feels more authentic because he is genuinely connected with skateboarding. 可以讲 Tony Hawk 出演的运动、滑板、游戏或品牌广告。核心是“可信度”：因为他真的代表滑板文化，所以广告不会显得只是硬找名人代言。

机智解决问题的人 Describe a person who solved a problem in a smart way Use his business response to a difficult period: instead of abandoning skateboarding, he created Birdhouse and built a business around the culture he already understood deeply. 可以讲行业低迷时，他没有简单放弃滑板，而是创办 Birdhouse，把自己真正了解的滑板文化转化成事业。这是一个“换一种方式解决生存问题”的角度。
给别人建议 Describe a time when you gave advice to others Say you used Hawk's story to advise a friend: choose something you genuinely enjoy, practice consistently, and do not expect progress without repeated failure. 可以讲你用 Hawk 的故事劝朋友：选择真正喜欢的事情，坚持练习，并接受“反复失败本来就是进步的一部分”。
最近读过的故事 Describe a story you have read recently Describe a biography, profile, or article about Tony Hawk's rise, early-1990s setback, 900, or business career. Treat it as a true-life story rather than fiction. 可以讲最近读到的一篇 Tony Hawk 传记、人物报道或文章，内容可以是成名、90 年代低谷、900 或商业经历。强调这是一个真实人物故事。
花费超过预期的物品 Describe an item on which you spent more than expected Talk about buying a skateboard, protective gear, or a Tony Hawk-related game after becoming interested in skating. Hawk is the reason for the purchase, not the main subject. 可以讲因为喜欢滑板或受到 Tony Hawk 影响，买了一块滑板、护具或相关游戏，结果比预算贵。这里 Hawk 是“购买动机”，不是题目主体。
完美工作 Describe your perfect job Bridge from Hawk's career to yourself: your ideal job could combine sport, media, game design, branding, or community work instead of being a traditional office job. 从 Hawk 的跨界事业转到自己：理想工作可以结合体育、媒体、游戏设计、品牌或社区项目，而不是传统坐办公室的工作。
想从事的短期海外工作 Describe a short-term job you want to have in a foreign country Say you would like to help at an international skateboarding event, skate camp, sports-media project, or community skatepark program in the U.S. 可以说想去美国短期参与国际滑板赛事、滑板训练营、体育媒体项目或社区滑板公园活动。Tony Hawk 的故事是你产生这个想法的原因。
外国的短期停留 Describe a foreign country you would like to stay and work for a short period of time Use the United States, especially Southern California, because of its skateboarding culture. Hawk can be your starting point, but the answer must stay focused on the country and what you would do there. 可以讲美国，尤其是南加州，因为那里滑板文化浓厚。Tony Hawk 可以作为你产生兴趣的起点，但主体必须仍然是“国家 + 你去那里做什么”。

C. Emergency / Slightly Forced Angles

Chinese Topic Name English Topic Name How to Use / 如何使用
早起经历 Describe a time when you got up early Say you woke up early to watch a skateboarding competition, livestream, or Tony Hawk-related event in another time zone. This works only if the timing sounds believable. 可以说为了看不同时区的滑板比赛、直播或 Tony Hawk 相关活动而早起。这个角度能用，但时间安排要编得合理。
在团队中工作 Describe a time when you worked in a group Describe doing a class presentation, video, or project about Tony Hawk, skateboarding culture, or the 900. The main focus must be teamwork and division of roles. 可以讲和同学一起做 Tony Hawk、滑板文化或 900 的课堂展示/视频项目。主体必须放在分工与合作，而不是人物传记。
发挥想象力 Describe a time you needed to use your imagination Say you designed a skatepark, poster, game level, or Tony Hawk-themed presentation and had to think creatively about layout, obstacles, or storytelling. 可以讲设计滑板公园、海报、游戏关卡或 Tony Hawk 主题展示，需要发挥想象力去设计路线、障碍或讲故事方式。
快乐活动 Describe a time when you organized a happy event successfully Use a skateboarding-themed birthday, game night, or class activity. You could play Tony Hawk's Pro Skater, watch famous tricks, and let friends try safe beginner activities. 可以设计一次滑板主题生日会、游戏夜或班级活动：玩 Tony Hawk 游戏、看经典动作，再安排一些安全的入门体验。
微笑的场合 Describe an occasion when many people were smiling Use a crowd celebrating a successful trick, a skatepark opening, or a shared viewing of Hawk's famous 900. Keep the smiles and atmosphere as the main focus. 可以讲大家看到某个高难度动作成功、滑板公园开幕，或者一起看 Hawk 的 900 时欢呼微笑。重点一定要放在“大家为什么开心”。
想有空时去旅游的地方 Describe a place you would like to visit in your free time Say you want to visit a famous skatepark or Southern California skateboarding location because Hawk's story made you curious about skate culture. 可以说想去著名滑板公园或南加州和滑板文化有关的地方，因为 Tony Hawk 的故事让你对真实的滑板文化产生兴趣。
去过的喜欢的城市 Describe your favorite city that you have visited San Diego can work only if you genuinely have enough to say about the city itself. Mention Hawk and skate culture as one small personal connection, not the whole answer. 如果你确实对 San Diego 有足够内容，可以把它和 Hawk、滑板文化联系起来。但城市本身必须是主体，不能整篇都在讲 Tony Hawk。
自行车/摩托车/汽车旅行 Describe a bicycle/motorcycle/car trip you would like to go on Use a road trip to a famous skatepark, skateboarding event, or Southern California location connected with the sport. This is usable but clearly a backup angle. 可以设计成开车去著名滑板公园、滑板赛事或南加州滑板地点的 road trip。能套，但人物联系较远，只适合备用。
想拥有的科技产品 Describe a piece of technology you would like to own Use a game console, handheld, or VR device mainly because you want to play a Tony Hawk skateboarding game. Keep the technology features as the real focus. 可以讲想要一台游戏机、掌机或 VR 设备，因为想玩 Tony Hawk 的滑板游戏。但真正主体必须是科技产品的功能和使用体验。
