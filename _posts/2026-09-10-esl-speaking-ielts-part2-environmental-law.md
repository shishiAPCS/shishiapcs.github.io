# 想要颁布的环保法律

# Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce

**You should say:**

* What it is
* When or where you first experienced it
* What is special about it
* And explain why it is memorable or important to you

---

# Stricter Carbon-Emission Regulations

# 更严格的碳排放法规

---

## A. Story Snapshot

## 故事概览

**One-line summary:**

I would like my country to introduce stricter regulations on carbon emissions. I first became interested in the idea through Bill Gates, but a video about a serious environmental disaster near the Nepal–China border made the issue feel much more real to me.

我希望国家出台更严格的碳排放法规。我最初是通过 Bill Gates 对这个话题产生兴趣的，但后来看到一段关于尼泊尔—中国边境严重环境灾害的视频以后，这个原本比较抽象的问题突然变得非常真实。

**Best for these topics:**

* **Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce**
* **Describe a new law you would like to introduce in your country**
* **Describe a law on environmental protection**
* **Describe an interesting video** — 把重点从“法律”切换到 Nepal–China border 的视频

**Logic path:**

```text
LAW
stricter carbon-emission regulations
        ↓
HOW?
EV subsidies + cleaner energy + stricter rules for companies
        ↓
FIRST INSPIRATION
Bill Gates / clean-energy technologies
        ↓
"But at that point, it was still abstract."
        ↓
REAL EVENT
video about the Nepal–China border disaster
        ↓
IMPACT
environment → people's homes and lives
        ↓
BACK TO THE LAW
reduce emissions + encourage cleaner choices
```

**Emotion path:**

curious → abstract → shocked → concerned → convinced

---

## B. Story Skeleton

## 故事骨架

**The law:** stricter regulations on carbon emissions  
（法律：更严格地限制碳排放）

**How it could work:** subsidies for EV purchases + stricter rules for companies to switch to cleaner energy  
（怎么执行：补贴新能源汽车购买 + 要求企业逐渐转向更清洁的能源）

**First inspiration:** reading about Bill Gates and clean-energy technologies he has supported  
（最初灵感：读到 Bill Gates 和他支持的清洁能源技术）

**Turning point:** watching a video about the devastating floods and mudslides near the Nepal–China border in late August 2026  
（转折点：看到 2026 年 8 月下旬尼泊尔—中国边境严重洪水和泥石流的视频）

**What I realized:** environmental problems do not only affect nature; they can directly affect people's homes and lives  
（真正意识到：环境问题不仅影响自然，也会直接影响人的生活、家园甚至生命）

**Conclusion:** support stricter carbon-emission laws and make greener choices easier and more affordable  
（结论：支持更严格的碳排放法规，同时让清洁能源和绿色出行更容易、更便宜）

## C. From Recording 1 to Recording 2

## 两次录音以后，我到底改了什么？

> 第一遍和第二遍最大的差别，并不是第二遍突然用了很多“高级词汇”。
>
> 真正的变化是：**第二遍开始出现一条清楚的逻辑线。**

### Version 1｜第一次回答的问题

第一次其实已经有不少好材料：

```text
carbon-emission law
    ↓
Bill Gates
    ↓
Nepal disaster
    ↓
glaciers
    ↓
people losing homes
    ↓
clean energy / EVs
```

问题是：**每一个点都不错，但是点和点之间的关系没有说清楚。**

听的人会不断问：

* Bill Gates 为什么突然出现？
* Nepal 的灾害和前面的 clean energy 有什么关系？
* 为什么从 landslide 又跳到了 glaciers？
* 最后为什么又突然回到 EV subsidies？

所以第一遍的问题不是“没内容”，而是：

> **有很多点，但是没有一条线。**

<details markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Version 1｜第一次录音（仅转写）</strong></summary>

Well, in our country, I guess I would like to have regulations about reducing carbon emissions, because I think it's such a big and important problem worldwide.

I first learned about this through reading about Bill Gates. He was pushing clean-energy ideas, and learning about his work made me start thinking about how important this issue is.

Then I thought about what happened recently near Nepal and Tibet. There was a major landslide, and the area was badly affected by mud and debris.

It is getting very challenging for us to keep temperatures low enough in high-altitude areas so that the ice on top of the mountains does not melt. There is also a subtle balance between the environment and people's lives.

After the incident, many people became homeless. That's why I think we need to cut down our carbon emissions, probably by adopting more clean energy and giving people enough subsidies to buy EVs.

</details>

---

### Version 2｜第二遍为什么明显更顺？

第二遍最重要的变化，是加入了一个非常好的转折：

> **But at that point, it was still quite an abstract idea to me.**

这句话把前后两段真正连起来了：

```text
Bill Gates
    ↓
I understood the idea intellectually.
    ↓
BUT it still felt abstract.
    ↓
I watched a real disaster.
    ↓
Now the environmental problem felt real.
```

第二遍也更早说明了这条法律到底怎么执行：

```text
carbon-emission regulations
├─ subsidies for EV purchases
└─ stricter rules for companies
      ↓
   cleaner energy
```

所以听众从一开始就知道：

> **你不是只在谈“环保很重要”，而是在描述一条具体可以怎么执行的法律。**

<details markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Version 2｜第二次录音（仅转写）</strong></summary>

Well, one environmental law I'd like my country to introduce and enforce is cutting down carbon emissions.

There are many different ways to do this, probably through subsidies for EV purchases or stricter rules for companies to switch to cleaner energy.

I first got this idea when I was reading about Bill Gates and some of the new technologies he has supported.

But that was still just an abstract idea. It didn't really feel personal until I watched a video about what happened near Nepal and Tibet. There was a major landslide recently, and a lot of people lost their homes and lives.

It reminded me how fragile our environment is, especially in high-altitude areas, where glaciers and ice can be extremely unstable and vulnerable.

And it doesn't just affect the environment. It also affects the people living in those areas.

That's why I think it's very important for our country to strengthen laws on carbon emissions.

</details>

### 两遍对比

| 第一遍 | 第二遍 |
|---|---|
| 一开始只说“减少碳排放” | 一开始就补充 **EV subsidies / company rules** |
| Bill Gates 和 Nepal 像两个独立例子 | 用 **abstract → real** 把两部分连起来 |
| 环境、冰川、灾害、人的关系比较跳 | 开始出现 **environment → people** |
| 最后突然回到 EV / clean energy | 结尾重新回到 **carbon-emission law** |
| 好点子很多，但连接词不够 | 信息之间出现明显的因果和转折 |

### Final revision｜最后还需要修什么？

第二遍已经明显更好，但还可以做三个小调整。

#### 1. Bill Gates 不要讲太长

Bill Gates 在这个故事里的作用只是：

> **He got me interested in the issue.**

不需要把他的所有项目都讲出来，否则会变成另一个故事。

#### 2. 不要把一次灾害简单说成 “climate change caused it”

更安全、更准确的表达是：

> **It made me think about how rising temperatures can make already fragile mountain environments even more vulnerable.**

重点是 **made me think about**，而不是强行证明单一因果。

#### 3. 最后一句一定要“落回题目”

不要在最后继续开新观点。

最简单的收尾就是：

> **That's why I'd strongly support stricter carbon-emission laws in my country.**

## E. Master Story: Angle Shift Table

## Master Story 话题切换表

这篇故事在 2026 年 9–12 月题库里最强的价值，不只是这一道“环保法律”。

真正可以复用的是两条支线：

```text
MASTER STORY
├─ LAW branch
│  ├─ environmental law
│  ├─ new law
│  └─ law on environmental protection
│
├─ VIDEO branch
│  └─ Nepal–China border disaster video
│
└─ PERSON branch
   └─ Bill Gates / clean-energy interest
```

| 题库话题 | 适配度 | 怎么切换重点 | 中文思路 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce** | ★★★★★ | 完整使用原故事 | 法律 → 执行方式 → 灵感 → 现实事件 → 人的影响 |
| **Describe a new law you would like to introduce in your country** | ★★★★★ | 开头去掉 environmental，后面基本不变 | 把 carbon-emission regulation 当作“新法律” |
| **Describe a law on environmental protection** | ★★★★★ | 强调这是一类 environmental protection law | 重点放在减排、清洁能源、长期影响 |
| **Describe an interesting video** | ★★★★★ | Nepal–China border video 变成主角；Bill Gates 和法律缩短 | 视频内容 → 为什么震撼 → 学到了什么 → 改变了什么想法 |
| **Describe someone who is older than you that you admire** | ★★★☆☆ | Bill Gates 变成主角；灾害只作为“为什么他的工作重要”的背景 | 人物 → 如何知道他 → clean energy → 为什么敬佩 |
| **Describe a piece of local news that people are interested in** | ★★☆☆☆ | **不建议硬套原故事**，除非你本来就在讲自己所在地区的相关事件 | Nepal–China border 对大多数学生来说不是 local news |

### 最强的新题适配：Describe an interesting video

如果抽到：

> **Describe an interesting video**

不需要重新准备一个故事。

只需要把结构从：

```text
LAW
↓
Bill Gates
↓
video
↓
impact
```

改成：

```text
VIDEO
Nepal–China border disaster
        ↓
WHAT I SAW
floods / mud / damaged homes
        ↓
WHY IT WAS INTERESTING
I had only understood climate issues in an abstract way before
        ↓
WHAT I REALIZED
environmental problems can become human problems
        ↓
IMPACT ON ME
I started thinking more seriously about emissions and clean energy
```

也就是说：

> **Master Story 不是背一篇答案去硬套所有题。**
>
> **Master Story 是保留同一批真实材料，但是根据题目改变“主角”。**

## F. Reference Version

## 精讲参考范文

Well, one environmental law I'd like my country to introduce and enforce is **stricter regulation on carbon emissions**.

我希望我们国家出台并加强执行的一项环保法律，就是**更严格的碳排放法规**。

There are several ways this could work. For example, the government could **provide more subsidies for EV purchases** and introduce stricter rules requiring large companies to **switch to cleaner energy sources**.

这项法律可以有几种不同的执行方式。比如，政府可以为购买新能源汽车提供更多补贴，同时出台更严格的规定，要求大型企业逐渐**转向更清洁的能源**。

I first became interested in this idea when I was reading about **Bill Gates** and some of the **clean-energy technologies** he has supported. His work made me realize that we need to find alternatives to traditional ways of producing and using energy.

我最初对这个想法产生兴趣，是因为读到了 **Bill Gates** 以及他支持的一些**清洁能源技术**。他的工作让我开始意识到，我们需要寻找传统能源生产和使用方式的替代方案。

But at that point, it was still **quite an abstract idea to me**. It didn't really feel personal until I watched a video about the devastating floods and mudslides near the **Nepal–China border** in late August 2026. The disaster followed a glacier collapse, and many people lost their homes and lives.

但在那个时候，这对我来说仍然是一个**比较抽象的概念**。直到后来，我看到了一段关于 2026 年 8 月下旬**尼泊尔—中国边境**严重洪水和泥石流的视频，我才真正觉得这个问题和现实生活联系了起来。那场灾害与冰川崩塌有关，很多人的家园和生活都受到了严重影响。

Of course, I can't say climate change was the only cause, but it made me think about how rising temperatures can make already **fragile high-altitude environments** even more vulnerable.

当然，我不能简单地说气候变化就是这次灾害的唯一原因，但这件事让我开始思考：不断升高的气温，会怎样让本来就**脆弱的高海拔环境**变得更加容易受到影响。

And what really struck me was that this **doesn't just affect the environment; it also affects the people living there**. Environmental problems can eventually become very serious human problems.

真正让我印象深刻的是，这些问题**并不只是影响环境，也会直接影响生活在那里的人**。环境问题最终也可能变成非常严重的人的问题。

So that's why I'd **strongly support stricter carbon-emission laws** in my country. I think encouraging clean energy and making greener choices more affordable could help reduce these risks **in the long run**.

所以，这就是为什么我会**强烈支持更严格的碳排放法律**。我认为，鼓励清洁能源、让更环保的选择变得更容易负担，从长远来看可以帮助我们降低这些风险。

## G. After Your First Recording

## 第一次录完以后，不要先改语法

录完自己的 Part 2，先不要急着逐句找语法错误。

先问自己下面3个问题：

1. **前 15 秒，听的人知道我到底要讲什么吗？**
2. **我的下一句话有没有增加新的信息？**
3. **两个材料之间的关系，我有没有明确说出来？**


这篇答案真正的改进过程可以压缩成一句话：

> **Don't add more ideas. Connect the ideas you already have.**

不要急着增加更多观点。  
先把你已经有的观点连接起来。
