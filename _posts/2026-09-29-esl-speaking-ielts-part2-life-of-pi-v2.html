IELTS Speaking Part 2 · Master Story · 2026 Sep–Dec

Life of Pi

《少年派的奇幻漂流》· 令人兴奋的一本书

Original Question · 原题

Describe an exciting book that you enjoyed reading.

You should say:

  • What kind of book it was
  • Why you chose to read it
  • What it was mainly about
  • And explain why you found it exciting

Learn one clear reading experience, then change the starting point, focus, and ending to answer many different Part 2 questions.

Full Answer

Use bilingual mode to understand it; switch to English only when you are ready to practice independently.

01

One exciting book that I really enjoyed reading was Life of Pi. A friend of mine recommended it to me first, but I was quite reluctant at the time because I didn't really enjoy reading novels. I mainly read nonfiction.

我非常喜欢的一本令人兴奋的书是《少年派的奇幻漂流》。最初是一位朋友推荐给我的，但当时我不太愿意读，因为我并不怎么喜欢小说，平时主要读非虚构类书籍。

02

Later, I came across the same book in a library, and I was drawn to its cover. I didn't plan to read it for long. I just wanted to skim through it and see what it was like. But after reading a few pages, I got really interested and ended up spending hours on it.

后来，我在图书馆偶然看到了这本书，被它的封面吸引住了。我本来没打算读很久，只想随手翻翻，看看它写得怎么样。没想到读了几页后，我就产生了浓厚的兴趣，结果一读就是好几个小时。

03

The book is basically a survival story about a boy named Pi who gets stranded on a lifeboat with a Bengal tiger. I found the idea incredibly intriguing because he not only has to survive at sea, but also has to figure out how to live alongside a dangerous animal without being eaten.

这本书主要讲的是一个叫派的男孩与一只孟加拉虎被困在救生艇上的求生故事。我觉得这个设定特别引人入胜，因为他不仅要在海上活下去，还得想办法与一只危险的动物共处，避免被它吃掉。

04

After I finished the book, I watched the movie adaptation with a group of friends during a movie night. To be honest, I was a little disappointed because I thought the novel was much better. When I read the book, I could understand what Pi was thinking and feeling through his inner monologue, and that's something that's quite difficult to show in a movie.

读完这本书后，我在一次电影之夜和一群朋友一起看了它的电影改编版。说实话，我有一点失望，因为我觉得小说好得多。读小说时，我能通过派的内心独白了解他的想法和感受，而这些在电影中很难表现出来。

05

Overall, I found the book exciting because the whole situation was so unusual and unpredictable. It also taught me an interesting lesson: sometimes something that seems dangerous or negative at first can actually help you survive or become stronger.

总的来说，我觉得这本书很精彩，因为整个处境非常不同寻常，而且难以预料。它还让我明白了一个有意思的道理：有时，一开始看似危险或负面的东西，反而能帮助你生存下来，或者变得更强大。

Topic Switcher

Core rule · 核心方法 Keep the same reading experience. Change the starting point, focus, and ending. Strong-fit topics can reuse the original story; adaptable topics need extra details.
★ Strong fit · 强适配 ↗ Adaptable · 可改编
★ Strong fit

令人兴奋的一本书

Describe an exciting book that you enjoyed reading

直接用原故事：推荐 → 图书馆偶遇 → 求生情节 → 电影对比 → 兴奋的原因。

Friend recommendation → library → survival → movie comparison → why it was exciting
★ Strong fit

有动物的故事 / 书

Describe a story/book with animals in it

重点放在 Bengal tiger：与 Pi 共处带来的危险、紧张感，以及老虎对求生的意义。

Make Richard Parker / the Bengal tiger the center of the answer.
↗ Adaptable

最不喜欢的电影

Describe your least favourite movie

突出期待与失望、小说的心理描写；需解释为什么是 least favourite，仅说 a little disappointed 不够。

Expectation → movie night → disappointment → inner monologue missing → why the film ranks poorly for you
↗ Adaptable

有趣的视频

Describe an interesting video you watched

补充看过 trailer / 幕后短片的经历；讲具体画面、内容和感受，书的情节只作背景。

Video becomes the main event; the novel becomes background context.
↗ Adaptable

喜欢去的建筑

Describe a building you enjoy visiting

主角换成 library；补充位置、外观、阅读区与常去的原因，偶遇这本书作为例子。

Library first → location / appearance / reading area → why you return → Life of Pi as one memorable visit
↗ Adaptable

重要决定 + 好结果

Describe an important decision that led to a positive result

决定认真尝试读小说；补充持续阅读带来的变化，解释为什么这个决定对你重要。

Reluctance → decision to try the novel seriously → new reading habit → positive result
↗ Adaptable

重要决定

Describe an important decision you made

仍用开始读小说的决定；重点放在犹豫、朋友的影响，以及最终下决心的过程。

Reluctance → friend's influence → decision → what happened after you started reading
↗ Adaptable

改变重要看法

Describe a time when you changed an important opinion

从 novels are not for me 到 novels can be fascinating；补充改变前后，并解释这对你的意义。

“Novels are not for me.” → Life of Pi → “Novels can be fascinating.”
↗ Adaptable

近期变化

Describe a recent change in your life

补充新习惯：睡前阅读代替刷手机；交代何时开始、如何坚持，以及对生活的影响。

Life of Pi as the trigger → reading before bed → less phone scrolling → effect on daily life
↗ Adaptable

长期目标

Describe a long-term goal you would like to achieve

把这本书作为起点；补充一年读几本英文小说、每周计划、困难及进度。

Life of Pi → new goal → number of English novels → weekly plan → difficulty + progress
★ Strong fit

和朋友度过的愉快夜晚

Describe an enjoyable evening you spent with friends

重点转到 movie night：补充地点、朋友和观后讨论；电影略失望，聚会仍然愉快。

Friends + place → movie night → reactions → discussion → why the evening was enjoyable
↗ Adaptable

帮助别人

Describe a time when you helped someone

补充向不爱读书的朋友推荐 / 借出这本书；交代对方的困难、你的帮助和结果。

Friend has a reading problem → recommend / lend Life of Pi → explain why → result
↗ Adaptable

好的购物 / 客服体验

Describe a good shopping experience / good customer service

另加书店购买经历；重点是店员如何推荐、找书或解决问题，不能只讲图书馆借书。

Bookstore visit → staff recommendation / finding the book / solving a problem → why the service was good
Mother material · 母素材
friend recommends → library → cover → skim → hooked → boy + tiger → survival → movie night → prefer the novel

习惯、目标、助人和购物属于可补充的延伸情节。
