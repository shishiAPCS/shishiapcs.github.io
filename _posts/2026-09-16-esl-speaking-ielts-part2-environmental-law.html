IELTS Speaking Part 2 · Master Story

环保法律：从一道题，长成一张 Master Story

这篇不是让你背一篇“万能答案”。先把原题答清楚，再看同一批真实材料为什么还能连接到 Video 和 Person 等其他 Part 2 题目。

1. 先看原题

第一次打开这篇帖子，先只想一件事：如果现在抽到这道题，我准备讲什么？

Describe an environmental law you would like your country to introduce
You should say:
  • What it is
  • When or where you first experienced it
  • What is special about it
  • And explain why it is memorable or important to you
我的答案中心：更严格的碳排放法规。
最初只是通过 Bill Gates 和 clean energy 对这个问题产生兴趣；后来看到一段真实灾害视频以后，这个原本很抽象的问题突然变得具体。
思路怎么长出来 Master Story 地图 原题故事骨架 怎么换题 两次录音怎么改 表达与发音 完整参考版本

2. 我是怎么从这道题想到这些内容的？

Master Story 不是先设计一张大网，再往里面塞题目。它通常从一道题开始，一步一步长出来。

题目：环保法律→ 我想解决什么？
carbon emissions→ 法律怎么落地？
EV subsidies + company rules→ 我为什么会关注？
Bill Gates / clean energy→ 什么让我真正有感觉？
disaster video
关键不是“素材多”，而是出现了一条认识变化：
Bill Gates 让我理解这个议题；真实灾害让我感受到这个议题。
这条 abstract → real 的变化，才把几个材料连成了同一个故事。

4. 先把原题答好：Environmental Law 故事骨架

先不要急着想还能套什么题。原题本身必须先有一条完整、好记的主线。

1
我要推出什么法律？stricter regulations on carbon emissions
2
它怎么执行？EV subsidies + stricter rules for large companies to switch to cleaner energy
3
我为什么开始关注？reading about Bill Gates and clean-energy technologies
4
当时的问题是什么？I understood the idea, but it still felt abstract.
5
什么让我真正意识到问题？a video about a serious disaster near the Nepal–China border
6
最后为什么支持这条法律？environmental problems can directly affect people's homes and lives
情绪线： curious → abstract → shocked → concerned → convinced

5. 同一个故事，怎么换题？

材料可以复用，但答案中心必须跟着题目改变。

⚖️ 如果题目是 Law 让“法律”一直当主角。
LAW→ 怎么执行→ Bill Gates 带来灵感→ 灾害让问题变真实→ 为什么支持
🎥 如果题目是 Interesting Video 视频成为主角；法律只放到最后，作为“它改变了我什么想法”。
VIDEO→ 看到了什么→ 为什么震撼→ 我意识到什么→ 它改变了我的想法
👤 如果题目是 Older Person You Admire Bill Gates 成为主角；灾害只负责说明为什么 clean-energy work 对我来说有意义。
PERSON→ 我怎么知道他→ 他关注什么→ 为什么这件事重要→ 为什么我敬佩他

适配度一眼看懂

Environmental law★★★★★

完整使用法律主线。

New law★★★★★

把 carbon-emission regulation 直接当作“想推出的新法律”。

Law on environmental protection★★★★★

强调减排、clean energy 和长期影响。

Interesting video★★★★★

把灾害视频变成主角，法律退到“后续影响”。

Older person you admire★★★☆☆

Bill Gates 变成主角；灾害只作为背景。

Local news★★☆☆☆

通常不要硬套，除非事件确实与你所在地区有关。

6. 从第一版到第二版：我是怎么把答案说顺的？

这里不是比较谁用了更多“高级词”。重点是让你看到：同样的材料，怎样从“很多点”变成“一条线”。

Version 1｜第一次回答

law→ Bill Gates→ Nepal disaster→ glaciers→ people→ EVs

问题：每一个点都不错，但听众需要自己猜它们为什么有关。

→

Version 2｜第二次回答

LAW→ HOW→ Bill Gates→ abstract→ REAL EVENT→ human impact→ BACK TO LAW

变化：没有增加很多新内容，而是补上了“怎么执行、转折、回扣”。

查看 Version 1｜第一次录音逐字稿

Well, in our country, I guess I would like to have regulations about reducing carbon emissions, because I think it's such a big and important problem worldwide.

I first learned about this through reading about Bill Gates. He was pushing clean-energy ideas, and learning about his work made me start thinking about how important this issue is.

Then I thought about what happened recently near Nepal and Tibet. There was a major landslide, and the area was badly affected by mud and debris.

It is getting very challenging for us to keep temperatures low enough in high-altitude areas so that the ice on top of the mountains does not melt. There is also a subtle balance between the environment and people's lives.

After the incident, many people became homeless. That's why I think we need to cut down our carbon emissions, probably by adopting more clean energy and giving people enough subsidies to buy EVs.

第一次以后，我发现的 4 个问题

  • 法律太抽象：只说“减少碳排放”，但没有很早说明这条法律具体怎么执行。
  • Bill Gates → Nepal 跳得太快：两个材料像两个独立例子。
  • landslide → glaciers → people 的关系不够清楚：听众要自己补逻辑。
  • 结尾突然回到 EV：没有明确落回“为什么我支持这条法律”。
第二遍最关键的一句桥：
But at that point, it was still quite an abstract idea to me.
它把“我知道这个问题”变成“但我还没有真正感受到这个问题”，于是后面的真实事件就有了存在的理由。
查看 Version 2｜第二次录音逐字稿

Well, one environmental law I'd like my country to introduce and enforce is cutting down carbon emissions.

There are many different ways to do this, probably through subsidies for EV purchases or stricter rules for companies to switch to cleaner energy.

I first got this idea when I was reading about Bill Gates and some of the new technologies he has supported.

But that was still just an abstract idea. It didn't really feel personal until I watched a video about what happened near Nepal and Tibet. There was a major landslide recently, and a lot of people lost their homes and lives.

It reminded me how fragile our environment is, especially in high-altitude areas, where glaciers and ice can be extremely unstable and vulnerable.

And it doesn't just affect the environment. It also affects the people living in those areas.

That's why I think it's very important for our country to strengthen laws on carbon emissions.

真正的改进，其实只有三类

① 具体化
把“减少碳排放”变成 EV subsidies + company rules + cleaner energy。听众终于知道这条法律到底怎么工作。
② 加桥梁
用 abstract → real 把 Bill Gates 和灾害视频连起来。两个材料不再是散点。
③ 收回题目
最后不继续开新观点，而是直接回到 why I support stricter carbon-emission laws。
还有一个事实表达上的精修：不要把一次具体灾害简单说成 “climate change caused it”。更稳妥的是：
It made me think about how rising temperatures can make already fragile mountain environments even more vulnerable.
Don't add more ideas. Connect the ideas you already have.

7. 必备表达：按“功能”记，不按单词表记

先知道这句话在故事里负责什么，再记英文。这样换题时才真的能复用。

⚖️ 提出法律 / 怎么执行

stricter regulations on carbon emissions
更严格的碳排放法规

provide subsidies for EV purchases
为购买新能源汽车提供补贴

switch to cleaner energy sources
转向更清洁的能源

🧠 讲“认识变化”

become interested in an issue
开始关注一个议题

it still felt quite abstract to me
当时对我来说仍然比较抽象

make the problem feel much more real
让问题突然变得具体、真实

🌍 讲环境与人的影响

a fragile high-altitude environment
脆弱的高海拔环境

become increasingly unstable and vulnerable
变得越来越不稳定、越来越脆弱

it doesn't just affect the environment; it also affects...
它不只是影响环境，也会影响……

🎯 收尾 / 回扣题目

strongly support stricter laws
强烈支持更严格的法律

make greener choices more affordable
让更环保的选择更容易负担

in the long run
从长远来看

重点词汇发音 💬

练法：听一次 → 跟读一次 → 马上用这个词组说一个完整句子。

8. 最后再看完整参考版本

先试着只看前面的 Story Skeleton 说一遍，再打开这里。不要把完整范文当成第一步。

Well, one environmental law I'd like my country to introduce and enforce is stricter regulation on carbon emissions.

我希望我们国家出台并加强执行的一项环保法律，就是更严格的碳排放法规。

There are several ways this could work. For example, the government could provide more subsidies for EV purchases and introduce stricter rules requiring large companies to switch to cleaner energy sources.

这项法律可以有几种不同的执行方式。比如，政府可以为购买新能源汽车提供更多补贴，同时要求大型企业逐渐转向更清洁的能源。

I first became interested in this idea when I was reading about Bill Gates and some of the clean-energy technologies he has supported. His work made me realize that we need to find alternatives to traditional ways of producing and using energy.

我最初对这个想法产生兴趣，是因为读到了 Bill Gates 以及他支持的一些清洁能源技术。

But at that point, it was still quite an abstract idea to me. It didn't really feel personal until I watched a video about the devastating floods and mudslides near the Nepal–China border in late August 2026. The disaster followed a glacier collapse, and many people lost their homes and lives.

但当时这对我来说仍然比较抽象。后来看到一段关于尼泊尔—中国边境严重洪水和泥石流的视频以后，这个问题才真正和现实生活联系起来。

Of course, I can't say climate change was the only cause, but it made me think about how rising temperatures can make already fragile high-altitude environments even more vulnerable.

当然，我不能简单地说气候变化就是这次灾害的唯一原因，但它让我开始思考：升高的气温会怎样让本来就脆弱的高海拔环境变得更容易受到影响。

And what really struck me was that this doesn't just affect the environment; it also affects the people living there. Environmental problems can eventually become very serious human problems.

真正让我印象深刻的是，这些问题不只是影响环境，也会直接影响生活在那里的人。

So that's why I'd strongly support stricter carbon-emission laws in my country. I think encouraging clean energy and making greener choices more affordable could help reduce these risks in the long run.

所以这就是为什么我会强烈支持更严格的碳排放法律。从长远来看，鼓励清洁能源并让绿色选择更容易负担，会是更实际的方向。

复习时只记这条线：
LAW → HOW → Bill Gates → abstract → REAL EVENT → human impact → BACK TO LAW
