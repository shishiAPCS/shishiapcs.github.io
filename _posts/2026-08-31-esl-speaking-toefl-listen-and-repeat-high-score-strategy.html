TOEFL Speaking：Listen and Repeat 高分策略

TOEFL Speaking: Listen and Repeat — High-Score Strategy

给已经熟悉题型、目标 5+ 的同学：少丢分，把更多单句从 3/4 提到 4/5。

For students who already know the task and are aiming for 5+: lose fewer points and turn more 3s/4s into 4s/5s.

依据 ETS 当前官方 TOEFL iBT Test Overview、Speaking Lesson Plan 与评分标准整理

Verified against current official ETS TOEFL iBT materials and scoring guidance

核心目标：更多句子拿 5；失误时尽量保住 4。
Core goal: turn more sentences into 5s; when something goes wrong, protect the 4.

一、先把题型事实记准

1. Know the task facts

7每组 7 个句子7 sentences per set
1×每句只听一次Each sentence is heard once
8–12s每句最长录音时间Maximum recording time per sentence
↑句子逐渐变长、变复杂Sentences get longer and more complex
👀屏幕图片与场景内容对应The visual corresponds to the scenario
✓目标：准确复述 + 清楚可懂Goal: accurate + clearly intelligible

不要混淆两套分数：Listen and Repeat 的每个单句按 0–5 分评分；你的 TOEFL Speaking 单科成绩则使用 1–6 分制（0.5 为一个档）。

Do not confuse the two scales: each Listen and Repeat item is scored 0–5, while the TOEFL Speaking section score uses the 1–6 scale in half-band increments.

High-score mindset: always aim for 5 first. If a small error happens, let the rubric protect the 4. Do not turn “what a 4 can tolerate” into a strategy for intentional omission.

三、真正能把 4 提到 5 的策略

3. Strategies that help turn 4s into 5s

1）按“意义语块”听，不要按单词一个个硬记

1) Listen in meaning chunks, not as a string of isolated words

长句如果在脑中变成二十个零散单词，会非常难保住；把它快速组织成几个有意义的块，更容易处理和复现。

A long sentence is much harder to retain if it feels like twenty unrelated words. Organize it into a few meaningful chunks so the language is easier to process and reproduce.

不要这样想：ensure / your / name / on / the / form / matches / the / records / before / submitting
Avoid processing it like this: ensure / your / name / on / the / form / matches / the / records / before / submitting

ensure your name / on the form / matches the records / before submitting

注意：“分块”是为了帮助你保住更多原词，不是为了删掉某些块。

Important: chunking is for retaining more of the original wording, not for deleting chunks.

2）利用屏幕图片：先锁定场景，再跟着视觉提示走

2) Use the visual: lock onto the scenario and follow the cue

ETS 明确说明，每组句子都有对应场景图，而且随着句子推进，视觉内容会移动到插图中相关的位置。图片能帮你更快确认地点、人物、物品、动作和顺序。

ETS states that each set has a visual representation of the setting, and progress through the sentences corresponds to movement through related parts of the illustration. Use it to anchor place, people, objects, actions, and sequence.

图片的作用：帮助你回忆和理解原句。
图片不是：让你自己编一个“意思差不多”的新句子。

The visual helps you: understand and retrieve the original sentence.
It does not: give you permission to invent a new sentence with roughly the same meaning.

3）不记笔记：把注意力全部留给这一遍音频

3) No notes: give the one listen your full attention

Listen and Repeat 每句话只播放一次，而且没有正式准备时间。做这一题时，不要边听边写。写下来的通常只能是零碎关键词，而你的真正目标是尽可能保住原句的完整语言。

In Listen and Repeat, each sentence is heard only once and there is no formal preparation time. Do not write while you listen. Notes usually capture only fragments, while your real target is to retain as much of the complete original sentence as possible.

课堂统一策略：眼睛看图，耳朵听完整，手不要写。
EYES UP. EARS ON. NO NOTES.

Rule: keep your eyes on the visual and your attention on the whole sentence.
EYES UP. EARS ON. NO NOTES.

4）高分同学尤其要听见“小词”

4) High scorers need to hear the small words

很多 4 → 5 的差距，不是句子没听懂，而是漏了 a / the / to / of / with / for / your / our / and 这类虚词，或者漏了 -s / -ed 等语法标记。

For many strong students, the difference between 4 and 5 is not comprehension. It is losing small function words such as a / the / to / of / with / for / your / our / and, or grammatical markers such as -s / -ed.

ETS 的 4 分标准允许少量这类错误；但 5 分要求原句完全准确。所以：不要因为“小词不影响大意”就忽略它们。

ETS allows a small number of such errors at score 4, but score 5 requires exact repetition. So do not ignore a word just because it seems unimportant to the overall meaning.

5）错一个地方，不要把整句推倒重来

5) One mistake? Do not restart the whole sentence

ETS 的 4 分描述明确允许考生出现自我修正，前提是最终成功完成回答。最安全的做法是：

The ETS score-4 description explicitly allows self-correction as long as the response is successfully completed. The safer approach is:

局部修正：log out— log into the portal and check your records.
Local correction: log out— log into the portal and check your records.

原则：局部改，继续往后；不要为了一个词把整句重新说一遍。

Rule: fix locally and keep moving; do not restart the whole sentence because of one word.

6）忘了一部分：先保住原意、实词和完整句

6) If part of the sentence disappears, protect meaning, content words, and sentence completeness

如果无法完全回忆，千万不要因为一个片段忘了就停住。根据 ETS 的 3 分标准，你仍然需要保住大多数实词或主要意思，并形成一个完整句。

If you cannot recall everything, do not stop because one piece is missing. ETS's score-3 description still expects a majority of the content words or ideas and a full sentence.

  1. 保原意：不要把关键信息说反。
  2. Protect the meaning: do not reverse or distort key information.
  3. 保实词：人物、动作、地点、物品、关键修饰信息能保多少保多少。
  4. Protect content words: retain as many key people, actions, places, objects, and modifiers as possible.
  5. 保完整句：不要只剩几个碎片。
  6. Protect sentence completeness: do not collapse into a few fragments.
  7. 一定完成：一个小漏词不要变成后半句全部消失。
  8. Finish the response: one missing word should not cause you to lose the entire second half.

7）清楚比“抢快”重要

7) Clear beats rushed

ETS 同时看准确度和可懂度。3 分描述中特别提到：卡在词或短语上、把词全部黏在一起，会降低 intelligibility。每句最多有 8–12 秒录音时间，所以不要为了“马上说”把整句挤成一团。

ETS evaluates both accuracy and intelligibility. The score-3 description specifically notes that struggling over a word/phrase or running words together can reduce intelligibility. With up to 8–12 seconds to record, do not compress the sentence into an unclear rush.

高分节奏：听完整 → 短暂停顿中快速回放 → 清楚、自然地说完整。

High-score rhythm: hear the whole sentence → use the short pause for a quick mental replay → deliver it clearly and completely.

8）通过 Shadowing 练“听得见 + 说得清”

8) Use shadowing to improve both hearing and delivery

ETS 官方 Speaking Lesson Plan 把 shadowing / shadow reading 列为 Listen and Repeat 的三种学习方法之一，并指出它有助于练习发音、节奏和语调。

The official ETS Speaking Lesson Plan lists shadowing / shadow reading as one of three study techniques for Listen and Repeat and says it helps develop pronunciation, rhythm, and intonation.

练习时不要只追求“像不像母语者”，更重要的是借助跟读把词与词的边界、弱读、连读和语块节奏听清楚，让下一次单听一遍时能识别更多原词。

During practice, the goal is not merely to “sound native.” Use shadowing to hear word boundaries, reduced speech, linking, and chunk rhythm more clearly so that you can recognize more of the original wording on a single listen.

四、ETS 官方支持的练习循环

4. An ETS-supported practice cycle

ETS 的 Speaking Lesson Plan 为 Listen and Repeat 明确推荐三种练习：多听几遍、录下自己的回答、Shadowing。把它们组合起来，可以这样练：

The ETS Speaking Lesson Plan explicitly recommends three Listen and Repeat study techniques: listen multiple times, make recordings, and shadow read. Combine them into this cycle:

五、考场最后只记这 9 条

5. The 9 test-day rules

Listen → Repeat

Listen → Repeat

  1. 先进入场景：快速看懂人物、地点和任务。
  2. Enter the scenario: identify the people, place, and purpose quickly.
  3. 跟着图片走：把视觉提示当成记忆锚点。
  4. Follow the visual: use it as a retrieval anchor.
  5. 不要记笔记：眼睛看图，耳朵听完整，把注意力留给这一遍音频。
  6. No notes: keep your eyes on the visual and give the one listen your full attention.
  7. 按语块听：理解几个意义块，但目标仍是保住所有原词。
  8. Listen in chunks: process meaning groups while still aiming to keep every original word.
  9. 盯紧小词：a / the / to / with / our / and / -s / -ed。
  10. Catch the small words: a / the / to / with / our / and / -s / -ed.
  11. 目标永远是 5：不要主动删词或改写。
  12. Always aim for 5: do not intentionally omit or paraphrase.
  13. 别抢：清楚、完整比一开录音就冲出去更重要。
  14. Do not rush: clear and complete beats launching into the sentence too fast.
  15. 错一处继续：局部修正，不要整句重启。
  16. One error? Keep going: correct locally; do not restart globally.
  17. 忘了一点也别放弃：保原意、保实词、保完整句、说到底。
  18. Forgot something? Never quit: protect meaning, content words, sentence completeness, and finish.
