本组 Cultural Festivals 是新托福 Take an Interview 的四连问。我们用一组真实口述思路串联四题：云南火把节的亲身体验 → 想体验传统美食 → 分享中国书法 → 传统文化的传承价值。每题另提供一条适合中国高中生迁移的示范路线。

本课核心： 同一个话题可以连续谈四次，但每一题必须回应不同的提问角度：Q1 经历与感受，Q2 想做什么，Q3 分享什么，Q4 意义何在。听到下一题与上一题重复时，换视角、换动作、换具体细节，不必推翻前面的回答。每题以约 45 秒为练习参考，根据语速自然调整。
QUESTION 1Personal ExperienceFestival → Scene → Feeling / Reason QUESTION 2Things to TryActivity → Why → How / Detail QUESTION 3Share Your CultureWhat → Demonstration → Value QUESTION 4Cultural PurposePosition → Example → Why It Matters

阅读顺序：先看 Q1 / Q2 如何自然衔接，再逐题阅读「答题思路 → 两篇 Sample Answers → 核心词块」。可点击思路卡切换例子，英文范文支持一键复制。A 线是本次口述的润色版本；B 线是给学生的替代思路，不能冒充自己真实参加过的活动。

重点：Q1 说过活动，Q2 还能说什么？

不用刻意藏着细节。 Q1 已经提到看舞蹈、欣赏灯笼，不代表 Q2 就不能再提它们。关键是后面能否提供新的动作或层次，而不是用相同句子再说一遍。

Q1 · What I Experienced

说过去的经历：去了哪里、看到了什么、为什么喜欢。

watch / notice / feel

“I watched people dance around a huge torch in a village square.”

Q2 · What I'd Like to Try

说未来的意愿：想亲自尝试什么、为什么吸引你。

try / learn / take part / make

“Next time, I'd love to join the dance instead of just watching.”

Q1 已经说了…Q2 可以这样接变化在哪里？
看过狮舞I'd love to learn a few basic lion-dance moves.观看 → 亲自学
看过花灯I'd love to make a lantern myself.欣赏 → 动手制作
在节庆活动里吃过小吃I'd like to learn how the dishes are prepared.品尝 → 了解做法

自然接话句： I've seen it before, but I've never actually tried it myself. / Another thing I'd love to experience is... 不要求 Q1 和 Q2 必须谈同一个节日，题目没有限定这一点。

Q1 · PERSONAL EXPERIENCE用一个真实画面介绍节庆经历

Question 1：去过文化节吗？为什么喜欢？

I'd like to ask you some questions about cultural festivals. First, cultural festivals are events that celebrate traditions like food, music, art, or clothing from a certain culture. Have you ever been to one, or would you like to go to one? Why?
Direct answer→One vivid scene→Personal reaction

这是整理思路的参考顺序，不是需要逐句背诵的模板。

答题思路：先回答「去过没有 + 为什么」

开头直接说 Yes, I have 或 I'd love to。然后选一个最能被想象出来的画面。A 线讲云南村庄广场上的大型火把、人群跳舞和跟朋友一起观看；重点不是介绍所有民俗，而是让听众看到当时的场景。

B 线讲春节期间逛庙会。春节本身当然是传统节日，但“参加庙会、看狮舞”等具体活动，比仅说“在家吃年夜饭”更符合题目对 festival event 的描述。

A · Yunnan Torch Festival
village square → giant torch → dancing → relaxed atmosphere
B · Spring Festival Temple Fair
temple fair → performances → crowded and lively → memorable experience
准确表达：如果不确定正式节日名称，可以说 a local torch festival in Yunnan；不要为了听上去专业就断言自己参加的是特定民族的官方活动。

选一个你讲得出细节的节庆

点击不同选项，查看适合 Q1 的画面与原因。

两篇示范：A 根据本次真实口述润色；B 为常见学生场景的语言示范。不要为填满时间虚构经历。

Sample Answer 1 · A · Yunnan Torch Festival / 云南火把节

Yes, I have. Last year, I went to a cultural festival in a small village in Yunnan Province. It was a local torch festival, and there was this huge wooden torch in the middle of the village square, with colorful flags near the top. People were dancing around it, while I just sat nearby, watched the performances, and chatted with my friends over some drinks. I really enjoyed how relaxed and welcoming the whole experience felt. It was a memorable way to experience local traditions.

Sample Answer 2 · B · Spring Festival Temple Fair / 春节庙会

Yes, I have. I've been to a temple fair during Chinese New Year, and it was a lot of fun. There were traditional performances, including lion dances, and the whole place was decorated with colorful lanterns. I remember walking around with my family, trying local snacks, and watching the performers. What I enjoyed most was the lively atmosphere. Everyone seemed excited to be there, and it made me feel more connected to the traditions I grew up with.

Core Vocabulary & Chunks

重点学习能迁移到其他口语话题的表达，不追求词汇堆砌。

  • a village square — 村庄广场
  • a giant wooden torch — 巨大的木制火把
  • watch the performances — 观看表演
  • a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere — 轻松又友好的氛围
  • a temple fair — 庙会
  • feel connected to traditions — 感到与传统文化有连接

Q2 · THINGS TO TRY从「看到」切换到「想尝试」

Question 2：参加文化节，你想看什么、做什么？

Thank you. If you were to go to a cultural festival, what would you like to see or do there?
One activity→Why it interests me→What I would actually do

这是整理思路的参考顺序，不是需要逐句背诵的模板。

答题思路：讲「具体想做的事」，不是再介绍一次节日

A 线从 try local food 发展到 learn how it's made。重点在于传统家庭食谱可能没有写进食谱书、当地食材有地域特点，所以想亲眼看并亲手学。

B 线则从「看灯笼」延伸成「亲手做灯笼」。如果在 Q1 已经谈过春节，也可以不换节日，直接换动作与关注点。

A · Local Food
try dishes → family recipes → watch the cooking → learn one dish
B · Lantern Making
admire lanterns → make one → learn the craft → take it home
提分点：别停在 I want to try delicious food。再回答：为什么外地吃不到一样的味道？你会怎么参与？ 这些细节能把简单想法展开。

活动不够新？换成一个能亲手参与的动作

这些是独立的 Q2 活动方向；挑其中一个就能发展成完整回答。

Sample Answer 1 · A · Traditional Food / 家庭食谱

Well, if I had the chance to attend a cultural festival, the first thing I'd want to do is try the local food, especially traditional dishes made from recipes passed down through generations. Some of these recipes aren't even written down in cookbooks; they're simply taught within families. And it's often hard to recreate the same flavors outside the region because the dishes use fresh local ingredients. I'd love to watch how the food is prepared and, if possible, learn how to make one of those dishes myself.

Sample Answer 2 · B · Making Lanterns / 动手做灯笼

I'd love to try making a traditional lantern. I've seen beautiful lantern displays during festivals, but I've never made one myself. I think it would be fun to learn how people build the frame and put the decorations together. It would also give me a chance to talk with local craftspeople and understand the work behind something that looks so simple. And at the end, I'd have a handmade lantern to take home, which would make the whole experience more memorable.

Core Vocabulary & Chunks

重点学习能迁移到其他口语话题的表达，不追求词汇堆砌。

  • recipes passed down through generations — 代代相传的食谱
  • passed down within families — 在家族内部传承
  • not written down in cookbooks — 没有写进食谱书
  • recreate the same flavors — 还原同样的味道
  • fresh local ingredients — 新鲜的当地食材
  • watch how something is prepared — 看某物是如何制作的

Q3 · SHARE YOUR CULTURE把自己变成文化节的介绍者

Question 3：如果介绍中国文化，你会分享什么？

Interesting. If you were to participate in a cultural festival for your own culture, what would you want to share with other people and why?
What to share→Let people try it→Why it matters

这是整理思路的参考顺序，不是需要逐句背诵的模板。

答题思路：Share 不是只能分享照片或视频

这里的 share 主要是「把自己文化中的某种传统介绍给别人」。想象你在国际文化节上有一个小展位：桌上放什么？你会演示什么？外国游客能不能亲手体验？

A 线教人用毛笔写 福（good fortune），并解释春节贴福字的传统。B 线教人做一道简单的四川菜。两条线路都能清楚回答 what 和 why。

A · Chinese Calligraphy
write 福 → explain its meaning → take artwork home
B · Sichuan Cooking
demonstrate a dish → explain local flavors → connect people
措辞提醒：Chinese calligraphy is an important part of Chinese culture 比 unique to Chinese culture 更准确；日本、韩国等也有各自的书法传统。

想象你有一个文化展位：你会让别人亲手做什么？

可点击选择一种文化内容，看看如何从「物品」发展成「互动体验」。

Sample Answer 1 · A · Chinese Calligraphy / 中国书法

I'd probably introduce people to Chinese calligraphy. I could set up a small booth and show visitors how to use a traditional brush to write a simple Chinese character, like Fu, which means good fortune. In China, people often put that character on their doors during Chinese New Year. I like this idea because visitors wouldn't just watch a demonstration; they could try it themselves and take their work home. It's a bit like painting, except you're using brushstrokes to create characters that carry meaning.

Sample Answer 2 · B · Sichuan Food / 四川传统美食

I'd love to share some traditional food from Sichuan, where I'm from. I could set up a small cooking station and show visitors how to prepare a simple local dish. I'd explain how we use ingredients like Sichuan peppercorns to create different flavors, and then let people taste the result. I think food is a great way to introduce a culture because you don't need to speak the same language to enjoy a meal together. It would also give me a chance to share something personal about my hometown.

Core Vocabulary & Chunks

重点学习能迁移到其他口语话题的表达，不追求词汇堆砌。

  • set up a small booth — 搭一个小展位
  • Chinese calligraphy — 中国书法
  • the character Fu / good fortune — 「福」字 / 好运、福气
  • symbolize good fortune — 象征好运
  • take their work home — 把自己做的作品带回家
  • bring people from different backgrounds together — 让不同文化背景的人走到一起

Q4 · CULTURAL PURPOSE表明观点，解释传统如何被延续

Question 4：文化节只是为了好玩吗？

Great. Some people think cultural festivals are mainly just for fun, while others think that they serve an important purpose, like helping to keep traditions alive. What do you think?
My position→One concrete mechanism→Why it matters

这是整理思路的参考顺序，不是需要逐句背诵的模板。

答题思路：表达立场，还要讲清楚「怎么发生」

这题不是让你简单喊口号。A 线的核心观点很精彩：文化节像是让我们短暂走进祖辈的生活。为了使观点更有说服力，继续补一句机制：孩子参加 → 记住传统 → 长大以后继续传承。

B 线从商业角度展开：节庆吸引游客，让手工艺人、传统食品经营者有机会靠自己的手艺获得收入，从而更有动力保留这些技艺。这条路线来自原始口述中的 business opportunity 思路，但需要把逻辑说完整。

A · Connection to Our Roots
take part → understand ancestors → remember → pass down
B · Support Local Traditions
visitors → income for artisans → continue traditional skills
平衡表达：承认节日的乐趣并不会削弱观点。Of course they're fun, but... 或 In fact, having fun can help keep traditions alive. 都非常自然。

选一个能解释清楚的论证角度

只选一条主线，然后补上「为什么会这样」；不要一次堆三个理由。

Sample Answer 1 · A · Keeping Traditions Alive / 文化传承

Well, I think cultural festivals are definitely more than just entertainment. They play an important role in keeping traditions alive. When people get together, they can enjoy traditional food, watch performances, and take part in customs that have been passed down for generations. In a way, it's like getting a glimpse into how our ancestors lived and celebrated special occasions. I think that matters, especially for younger people, because when they experience these traditions firsthand, they're more likely to remember them and pass them down in the future.

Sample Answer 2 · B · Fun Can Support Tradition / 乐趣与传承并不冲突

I actually think cultural festivals can be fun and meaningful at the same time. The entertainment attracts visitors, but that attention can also help keep traditions alive. For example, when people visit a festival, they might buy handmade crafts or try food prepared by local families. That gives craftspeople and food vendors a reason to continue practicing traditional skills. So even if people originally come just to have fun, they're still supporting the local culture. To me, that's one of the most valuable things about these events.

Core Vocabulary & Chunks

重点学习能迁移到其他口语话题的表达，不追求词汇堆砌。

  • more than just entertainment — 不只是娱乐
  • keep traditions alive — 让传统延续下去
  • get a glimpse into how our ancestors lived — 一窥祖辈过去的生活
  • experience something firsthand — 亲身体验某事
  • pass traditions down to future generations — 把传统传给后代
  • support local craftspeople — 支持当地手工艺人

四题一条线：从「我看见」到「文化为什么重要」

这一组题的优势是能够自然延续同一个文化话题，但不需要在每题重新讲节日背景。四个角度各自独立，回答时才不会越讲越重复。

1Experience我去过 · 我看到 · 我的感受
2Participation我想尝试 · 原因 · 具体怎么做
3Sharing我来介绍 · 互动体验 · 文化含义
4Purpose我的观点 · 具体例子 · 为什么有价值

45 秒自检：四道题各检查一个重点

  • Q1：我有没有明确回答是否去过？有没有一个具体场景和个人感受？
  • Q2：我说的是自己想做什么，还是只是罗列节庆通常有什么？
  • Q3：我是否说清楚自己能向其他文化的人展示、教或解释什么？
  • Q4：除了表达立场，我有没有解释「参加文化节」为什么能导致「传统被延续」？

课堂练习：一题说完，再突然播放下一题

  1. 从春节庙会、元宵灯会、端午龙舟赛等熟悉的活动中选一项，回答 Q1；如果没去过，就用 I'd like to... 诚实表达愿望。
  2. 老师不提前透露 Q2。听到后先想：这一次我能做什么新动作？ 比如 watch → join、taste → learn to cook。
  3. Q3 想象自己有一个文化展位：选一项可以演示的传统，说明游客会怎么参与，以及为什么值得分享。
  4. Q4 用一条完整因果链回答：活动发生 → 人们有什么体验 → 传统为何更容易传下去。

八篇范文都用于学习思路、词块与口语节奏，不是考试中必须背诵的答案。A 线源自本次讨论中的个人口述；B 线用于举一反三，具体经历应根据真实情况修改。
